Dental Implants in Windsor

Dental implants restore your beautiful smile. Not only do they provide a permanent base for restorations such as crowns, but they also help prevent bone loss that results when you lose a tooth.

How implants work

Dental implants are made from titanium alloy. This unique metal bonds with your bone to form a permanent and natural bond. The implant is surgically placed in your jawbone, where it heals and bonds with the bone. Once it has healed, we can attach a restoration such as a crown, bridge, or even dentures to the implant.

Mini implants

Mini implants are special implants that can be placed quickly. They’re used for certain restorations such as implant-mounted dentures.
