Dr. Christine Thibert

Dr. Thibert brings over 30 years of experience and a deep commitment to patient-centred care, aligning seamlessly with our dedication to providing top-quality dentistry in a friendly, welcoming environment.

A proud South Windsor native, Dr. Thibert’s journey into dentistry began at Assumption College High School and continued with a bachelor’s degree in biology from the University of Windsor. She went on to earn her dental degree from the University of Western Ontario in 1994. Since then, she’s focused on building meaningful relationships with her patients, ensuring they feel valued, heard, and cared for at every visit.

Beyond her dental expertise, Dr. Thibert is a part-time fitness coach passionate about wellness and enjoys an active lifestyle. In her personal time, she cherishes moments with her two sons, the loves of her life.

We’re delighted to have Dr. Christine Thibert join our team and continue providing the excellent care you’ve come to expect from our practice.
