Dr. Gabrielle Dionne

Dr. Gabrielle Dionne was born in Portland, Oregon, and moved to Tecumseh shortly thereafter. Given that her father is an orthodontist (Dr. Dennis Dionne), she was surrounded by dentistry from an early age. She attended École secondaire l’Essor, where she became fluent in French, followed by a year at Neuchâtel Junior College in Neuchâtel, Switzerland.

Dr. Dionne remained in Dublin for a few years following graduation, working both in general family practices as well as in an exclusively paediatric and orthodontic practice under the supervision of a specialist Paediatric Dentist.

Dr. Dionne is a family-focused, patient-first dentist who believes dental care should be comfortable, personalized, and even enjoyable. She especially enjoys working with children and patients who experience anxiety, taking the time to create a calm, welcoming environment built on trust and empathy. She is proud to offer care in both English and French. Her goal is for every patient to leave feeling heard, cared for, and confident in their smile.

Outside of work, she enjoys spending time with her family, sea swimming (though now that she’s home, it will be lake swimming!), hiking, trying new recipes, and pub trivia nights. She can’t wait to re-explore her hometown after so many years away and is delighted to be back.
