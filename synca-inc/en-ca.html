NEW Ligher Kepler and up to 7.6x

Highly esthetic NEW milling blocks

Synca now offers you Amber® Mill Direct from HASS — CAD/CAM blocks with superior physical properties made from pre-crystallized lithium disilicate that requires no firing. They enable true single-visit indirect restorations with lifelike color gradients.

NEW Wireless Digital Sensor

CADI® Optimum AIR converts x-rays directly into an electronic signal (direct-conversion); a technology that provides greater native sharpness vs. other sensors that must convert x-rays to visible light before creating an image.
