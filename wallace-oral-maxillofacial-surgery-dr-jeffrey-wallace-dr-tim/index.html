Le cabinet

Dr. Jeffrey Wallace has been providing care to residents of Waterloo, Kitchener and Cambridge for 10 years. Dr. Jeffrey Wallace provides care at his office as well as at St Mary’s General Hospital and Grand River Hospital.

Dr. Timothy Wallace, the founder of the practice, has been providing care for over 40 years and continues to see consultations.

Dr. Wallace’s goal is to provide every patient with personal, caring and comprehensive treatment. His interests include hiking, gardening, downhill skiing, travelling and spending time with his young family.

Upon receiving his Master of Science degree in Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery, Dr. Wallace started his practice in Waterloo in 1980, and over the past forty years has raised two sons, one of whom has followed in his footsteps as an Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeon.

Throughout his career, Dr. Wallace remained committed to the ongoing development of his profession and was actively involved in the Schulich School of Medicine and Dentistry at Western University where he was an Adjunct Professor, volunteering time to help train Ontario’s future dentists.
