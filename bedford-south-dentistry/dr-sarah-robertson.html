Dr. Sarah Robertson

Sarah was born in Ottawa, Ontario, and moved to Timberlea, Nova Scotia, during her middle school years. Having spent countless summers with her grandparents and extended family across New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Nova Scotia, she proudly considers herself an “honorary Maritimer.”

Sarah is delighted to be returning to Bedford South Dentistry as a general dentist. Prior to pursuing dentistry, she worked at the practice as a hygiene coordinator and receptionist, experiences that helped shape her passion for patient care and fostered a deep connection to the Bedford community.

Sarah earned her Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Biology from Acadia University before completing her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree at Dalhousie University in 2026. During dental school, Sarah was awarded the Dr. Don Stephenson memorial scholarship for achieving academic excellence and demonstrating outstanding abilities to treat her patient family.

Sarah looks forward to providing compassionate, patient-centered care.

Outside of dentistry, Sarah enjoys spending time with her fiancé and taking advantage of Nova Scotia’s beautiful trails, campgrounds, and beaches.
