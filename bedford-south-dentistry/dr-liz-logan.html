Dr. Liz Logan

Dr. Liz Logan was born and raised in Halifax, Nova Scotia. She stayed close to home for her undergraduate degree in Mathematics and Chemistry at Dalhousie University. She has always had a desire to travel, so following her undergraduate degree, she spent some time in Asia teaching children English as a second language.

It was always Dr. Liz’s plan to pursue a career in Dentistry. Following her travels, she enrolled in the Faculty of Dentistry at McGill University in Montréal, Québec and graduated with a Doctorate of Dental Medicine.

Dr. Liz enjoyed working with children during her training in dental school and was encouraged by her instructors to apply to the Pediatric General Practice Residency Program at the IWK Health Centre. She was accepted into the residency and began her career in children’s dentistry. She has been treating children in need in HRM for over a decade, and she is very excited to be continuing to offer sedation dentistry for patients between 4 and 25 years of age at Bedford South Dentistry.

Dr. Liz has been a clinical instructor at Dalhousie, and she has participated in the annual interview process for prospective dental students. She is a member of the Halifax County Dental Society, and the Nova Scotia Dental Association, where she is the topic monitor for Early Childhood Caries and First Tooth First Visit. Dr. Liz is also a member of the Canadian Dental Association and the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry.

In her spare time, Dr. Liz enjoys spending time with her husband, Dr. Ross Leighton, family, and their sweet Golden Retriever, Maple. She and her husband share quite a passion for skiing, sailing, and world travel.
