Bedford South Dentistry is located at 15 Peakview Way, in the Sobey’s plaza just off the 102 at the Larry Uteck exit. We offer plenty of free parking, and flexible hours—we open early every weekday, and close late two evenings a week.

To help make your visit as pleasant as possible, we invite you to lean back and watch TV in our waiting area and in the dentist’s chair—they’re massaging dental chairs that provide soothing massage therapy during your appointments!

Our dental practice is an oasis, with a warm, calming environment, decorated in earth tones and sleek furnishings. Fully equipped with state-of-the-art technology, Bedford South Dentistry has what it takes to provide high quality, comfortable care.

We direct bill insurance claims electronically, so you won’t need to bother with the hassle of submitting claims to your insurance company. We also accept cash, debit, cheque, VISA, and MasterCard. Payment for your visit is due at the time of your appointment.
