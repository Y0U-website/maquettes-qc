Dr. Jillian Reynolds

Dr. Reynolds—or Dr. Jill, as her patients call her—is originally from Grand Falls-Windsor, NL. She completed a Bachelor of Science at Saint Mary’s University in 2000, followed by a Doctor of Dental Surgery from Dalhousie Dental School in 2005. She worked at Trimac Dental Centre for six years before opening Bedford South Dentistry with her friend and classmate, Dr. Natalie Brothers, in Sept. 2011.

Dr. Jill is very friendly and outgoing, loves implant and cosmetic dentistry, and is known for having a great rapport with children.
