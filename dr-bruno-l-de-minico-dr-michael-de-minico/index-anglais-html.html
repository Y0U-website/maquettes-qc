Our services

At Clinique Dentaire De Minico we offer all the services you need to maintain an excellent oral health.

General and Preventive dentistry

Whether for a diagnostic examination with digital radiography, cleaning, whitening, fillings, removable and fixed dental prosthesis, or LASER gum treatments, count on us to get dental care for all your family members (children and adults), in a friendly and modern environment.

Crown, bridges and veneers (CEREC)

You need to cover a fragile tooth or replace one or more adjacent teeth? A crown, bridge or porcelain veneers are durable and aesthetic solutions. We also offer CEREC, a CAD-CAM technology that allows us to create a porcelain crown or onlay, on site, in one appointment! No impressions and laboratory wait time.

Root Canal Treatment Endodontics

Root canal treatment or endodontic treatment is a procedure that consists in cleaning the canals of the tooth in the presence of an infection, an abscess or necrosis of the nerve. In the center of the tooth is the pulp, which can become infected because of a trauma, deep decay or repeated fillings.

Oral Surgery / Implantology

We offer wisdom teeth extractions and implant placement to replace one of more missing teeth, as well as the placement of implant-stabilized removable prosthesis, bone grafts and gingival grafts.

Orthodontics (INVISALIGN)

We offer different approaches and various treatments to move teeth, whatever your age. We offer among others, Invisalign, a removable transparent aligner system. Replaced every 2 weeks, Invisalign gradually moves teeth without braces and wires.

Whitening

Your smile allows you to make your best first impression. Achieve results in less than one hour with Philips Zoom or with LASER in-office treatments, or with Zoom take-home whitening with custom-made trays that you wear a few minutes a day for 1-2 weeks, depending on the concentration and wear time. Try Zoom whitening and find a brighter smile!
