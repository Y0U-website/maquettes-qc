Ceramic crowns

Full Zirconium Multi Layer

Zirconium is a very strong material with an excellent flexural strength of more or less 1000 Mpa, which explains its great popularity with posterior crowns and bridges.

the zircon is milled at green stage with the help of CAD CAM technology, it can be pre-shaded or custom-shaded

Indications: Posterior crowns and bridges and for grinders.

IPS e.max CAD

This innovative lithium disilicate glass-ceramic block for the CAD/CAM technology creates long-lasting all-ceramic restorations with impressive esthetic properties.

After milling, the IPS e.max CAD block is crystallized. This process yields the material’s final color, translucency and flexural strength of over 400 MPC with no shrinkage.

Indications: For esthetic cases, single crowns and thin veneers.

Laminated Zirconia Crown Bridge

Zirconia is highly durable with excellent flexural strength. However, it lacks opalescence. To achieve this quality, we apply a layer of Feldspathic ceramic to the labial facade of the zirconia, enhancing opalescence and characterizing the prosthesis to the desired level.

Signature Crowns

These high-end restorations, studied at the IOAD «Master of Anterior all ceramics». They are made with a lot of precision and require a special attention from our technicians, a personalized stratification of the porcelain, selection shade and special coloration if necessary.
