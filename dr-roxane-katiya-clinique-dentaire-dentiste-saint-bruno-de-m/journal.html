What is oil pulling and what is it for?

What is oil pulling? It is an ancient method of Ayurvedic medicine that has existed for at least 3000 years where oil is worked, pulled, pushed between the teeth and on the gums to clean the mouth.

ALL-ON-4 or ALL-on-6?

The ALL-ON-4 and ALL-ON-6 is a treatment where all the teeth are removed and replaced immediately with 4-6 implants so that you can leave the same day with your teeth. Sometimes the quality of bone does not allow us to place the fixed denture on the same day and the patient must leave with a removable denture.
