DENTAL SERVICES Complete and recall examinations, follow-ups, imaging, diagnosis

The dental examination includes a complete clinical assessment, including analysis of dental, periodontal and bone tissues, as well as diagnostic imaging when required.

Lhe diagnoses enable the development of prioritized treatment plans based on actual clinical needs and therapeutic priorities.

3D scanning allows for three-dimensional visualization of anatomical structures, which improves diagnostic accuracy and the planning of complex treatments.

DENTAL SERVICES Dental restorations, fillings and inlays

Restorations are performed according to principles of precision, seal, and durability, using materials tailored to each clinical situation. Options such as composite, ceramic, porcelain, and zirconia restorations are available and aesthetically pleasing, and are provided at our clinic according to your needs and expectations.

Fixed prostheses , including crowns, onlays, bridges and reconstructions, are planned to restore occlusal function, stability and structural integrity of the teeth.

DENTAL SERVICES Management of dental infections

Odontogenic infections are treated according to current clinical protocols in order to eliminate the infectious source and prevent spreading and deterioration.

Clinical follow-up is provided to confirm complete resolution of the condition.

Treatments for dental infections management may include: abscess drainage, root canal treatments, curettage, and others.

DENTAL SERVICES Management of jaw/tmj disorders

Temporomandibular disorders and myofascial pain are assessed according to clinical and functional criteria.

Occlusal stabilization appliances and nightguards are custom-designed to stabilize occlusion and reduce mechanical stress.

Extractions are performed according to safe surgical protocols, with appropriate pain and healing management.

Surgical indications are evaluated according to dental position, anatomical structures and associated risks.

Periodontal/gum disease is a chronic inflammatory condition that can lead to bone loss if left untreated.

Treatments visent à contrôler l’inflammation, stabiliser les tissus et maintenir la santé parodontale à long terme.
