General Services

St George Dental provides comprehensive dental care in one convenient location. Our friendly and experienced team is dedicated to keeping your smile healthy and beautiful.

This is a thorough exam that involves a full evaluation of check-up x-rays, inspection of your teeth for cavities, cracks, chipped teeth, missing teeth, discoloured teeth, and missing or broken fillings and checking your gums for signs of gum disease, oral cancer screening.

At St George Dental, we provide teeth cleaning to remove plaque, tartar, and stains to help you keep your teeth and gums healthy. Routine cleanings is the best way to prevent gum disease.

Our expert dentists in Toronto use white composite fillings to repair teeth that are damaged by cavities or fractures. The white filling material is carefully selected to match the colour of your existing teeth, providing you with a long-lasting and healthy smile.

Our dental professionals provide comfortable root canal treatment to alleviate the pain of an infected tooth and prevent the need for extraction. We provide the most up-to-date techniques and anaesthetics to ensure your comfort.

Wisdom teeth typically begin causing problems in late teens and young adulthood. If you develop pain due to infection or decay of the wisdom teeth, St George Dental dentists can help.

At St George Dental, we can help you achieve the smile you’ve always wanted. We offer many cosmetic dentistry services including teeth whitening, Six Month Smiles clear braces, Invisalign invisible braces, porcelain veneers, dental implants, and more.
