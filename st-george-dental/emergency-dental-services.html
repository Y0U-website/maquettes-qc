Book An Appointment Emergency Dental Clinic in Toronto Tooth pain can be excruciating but at St George Dental, we put your comfort and safety first. We provide immediate dental care for: Toothaches
  • Fractured or chipped teeth
  • Loose or lost fillings
  • Bleeding gums or gum pain
  • Dislodged crowns
  • Wisdom teeth pain
  • Swelling of the jaw or face
  • Dental infections

What You Can Do During a Dental Emergency

Call our Toronto dental office right away.

Ask for an emergency dental appointment and explain your situation and condition!

Follow the instructions provided by our operator and arrive at your scheduled appointment time and let our dentists do the rest.
