St George Dental

Full Service Dental Clinic in Toronto

At St George Dental, we are proud to serve the community near the University of Toronto in downtown Toronto. We have over 20 years of combined experience in complete dental care services for the whole family. Our team of dental professionals provide general and cosmetic dentistry with compassion, understanding, and a personalised approach.

Our patients are our most valuable asset and we work hard to make sure they have the best care possible. Our caring staff listens to your needs and will design a treatment plan to exceed your expectations, starting with education on procedures, treatments, products, and more, so you can make informed decisions regarding your oral health.
