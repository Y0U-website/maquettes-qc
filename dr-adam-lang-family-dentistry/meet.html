Meet Dr. Adam Lang

Family Dentistry

I graduated in dentistry from The University of Western Ontario in London, Ontario. I began practicing family dentistry for two years in New Liskeard, Northern Ontario, then moved back to my hometown area of Waterloo where I practiced in Cambridge for three years.

In my spare time, I enjoy riding my motorcycle and driving sometimes far, but always in search of good food. Beyond dentistry, my passions are history, geography, video games, computers and spending time with friends and family. I look forward to providing you and your family with many years of dental health care.
