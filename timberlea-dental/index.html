Le cabinet

Led by Dr. Azin Alborzi and a great team of experienced dentists. Our team balances technical excellence with patient-centred service. This means you’ll always get attentive service, lasting results and sound advice that takes into account your long-term health and wellbeing.

Come to us for regular check-ups, preventive treatments, restorations, cosmetic dentistry, oral health questions or dental emergencies. Whatever you need, we’re here for you. Our goal is to see you smile—today and tomorrow!

Advanced expertise and treatments Modern dentistry should be fast, comfortable and long-lasting. We do preventive dentistry (dental hygiene), simple and complex restorations, including crowns, implants, bridges, dentures, root canal treatments and gum care—are performed using the most advanced techniques and innovative technologies.

At Timberlea Dental Clinic, we firmly believe in patient education and preventive care. That’s why we pair innovative procedures with personalized recommendations and day-to-day oral care instructions. We’ll explain the root cause of each problem and which treatment options are suitable for you.

Making sure you’re well-informed is a pleasure and a responsibility we take seriously.

Service with a smile We may be serious about quality dentistry but rest assured that we’re lighthearted in our approach.

Count on us for friendly service, unhurried attention and a genuine desire to put you at ease. Who says that advanced dental care can’t be delivered with a healthy dose of good humor?

We want you to think of us as good friends that you don’t see often, but that stick with you for life.
