Dr. Soofia Shafiei

Dr. Soofia Shafiei was recognized as a dentist in Canada in 2024 and began her collaboration with McGill University Faculty of Dentistry and the Montreal General Hospital as a faculty lecturer. She obtained her Doctorate of Dental Surgery degree from Isfahan University of Medical Sciences in 1999 and successfully completed her specialty program in Prosthodontics at the same university in 2012.

Between 2015 and 2020, Dr. Shafiei served as an assistant professor in the Department of Prosthodontics at Isfahan University of Medical Sciences, the University of Dentistry, and Ahvaz Jundishapur University of Medical Sciences.
