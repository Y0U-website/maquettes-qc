Dr. Kwon Kim

He grew up in Stratford and completed dental school at University of Western Ontario, Schulich school of Medicine and Dentistry in 2012.

Dr. Kim is offering routine as well as advanced treatments, including implant surgery, bone and soft tissue grafting, wisdom teeth extractions, root canal treatments under surgical operating microscope and IV sedation.
