Frequently Asked Questions

Dental implants are a permanent tooth-replacement solution that involves the insertion of a titanium post into your jawbone, which is then topped with a custom dental crown. The implant integrates with the bone to provide a stable, natural-looking replacement tooth that functions like your natural teeth.

The majority of adult patients with good to excellent oral and general health are eligible for dental implants. Good candidates include those with healthy gums and enough jawbone density, although bacterial infection can destroy bone grafts.

If properly cared for and maintained, dental implants can be very durable and last upwards of 20 years! Most patients achieve long-term results, making implants a long-lasting, economical solution for missing teeth.

The dental implant process is often performed under local anesthesia, making it painless for the patient. It is normal to experience slight swelling or discomfort after surgery, which typically subsides within several days.

The dental implant procedure typically spans a few months from start to finish. This involves the first visit, implant placement, and the 2nd visit, the 3rd visit for placing the final crown.

Many people find themselves back to normal and sleeping in their own bed the night of surgery. Complete healing and bone growth are generally achieved within 3-6 months, depending on the patient’s healing rate and any other procedures that may have been necessary.

The price of dental implants in Ontario varies depending on how many are needed, what type, and sometimes on additional procedures like bone grafting. Consultations at King Street Dental Centre include customised treatment plans along with up-front pricing.

A few dental insurance plans provide limited coverage for implants or may help pay for minor work. Insurance coverage varies by provider, and the dental staff can assist with reviewing insurance benefits and financing options.

Dental implants need to be cared for almost like natural teeth; daily brushing, flossing, and dental checkups are all essential. Good oral hygiene will help prevent gum disease and maintain the longevity of your implants.
