An Orthodontist is a specialist in the diagnosis and treatment of dental and facial irregularities. Orthodontists must successfully complete an additional residency program of at least two-three years of advanced AND FULL TIME education in orthodontics after obtaining the dentist diploma.

This advanced training includes such diverse studies as genetics, embryology, human growth and development, and biophysics. In Canada, only dentists with this advanced specialty education can present themselves as Orthodontists.

The title Orthodontist in Montreal can only be granted by the Ordre des dentistes du Québec, which is the governing authority in Quebec.

Orthodontics is a recognized specialty in dentistry that diagnoses, treats and even prevents irregularity in the teeth and in the face. Orthodontics can treat and correct what is known as “bad bite” or malocclusion.
