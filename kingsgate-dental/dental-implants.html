Replacing teeth and restoring smiles with dental implants

One of the most significant advantages to dental implant treatment is the effect that replacement roots have on the jawbone. With standard replacement that sits on the gums, bone loss occurs quickly. While implants may not fully stop the degradation of bone, they slow the breakdown.
