Some of the Benefits of Straighter Teeth

Healthier gums Properly positioned teeth are easier to brush and floss than teeth that overlap, are misaligned, or too widely spaced. When teeth are properly aligned, the gums surround them better, which leads to better periodontal health (healthy gums).

Easier cleaning Proper dental hygiene helps reduce plaque buildup, tooth decay, and periodontal disease (gum disease).

Improved chewing and pronunciation In some cases, incorrect tooth and jaw location can cause speech and chewing difficulties. The correction of such an occlusion can greatly enhance pronunciation and chewing, therefore enhancing your quality of life.

Less risk of dental trauma and abnormal wear Correctly positioned teeth apply pressure on bones and their supporting mandibular joints, helping to reduce the risks of abnormal wear and dental trauma.

Healthy, natural smiles Invisalign technology will straighten your teeth and contribute to your self-confidence and comfort… in beautiful fashion!
