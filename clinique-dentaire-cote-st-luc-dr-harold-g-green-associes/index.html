Our mission is to provide comprehensive and compassionate dental care with a focus on lifelong relationships founded on respect, honesty, trust and dignity. Our dedicated team is committed to providing quality dental care in a comfortable, relaxed, friendly and fun environment.

Our hygienists (Melanie and Tracy) and dental assistant (Rene and Sharmilla) are registered dental professionals who pride themselves on achieving the best possible results for you and your family. Our office manager (Paula) and our administrative secretary (Debbie) are always here to greet you in a warm and friendly manner and will facilitate your visit from start to finish

Our ultimate desire at the Côte St-Luc Dental Clinic, is to be your partner in optimal oral health. We want to listen, ask questions and create awareness. By designing action plans and setting goals, our aim is for you to be empowered and we will strive to honour your trust.
