Are You a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants are a good choice for most adults who have good overall health and oral health. However, factors like smoking, certain medical conditions, and insufficient bone density may affect eligibility. A thorough evaluation by our dental team will determine if implants are right for you.

Maintaining dental implants requires the same level of care as your natural teeth. Regular brushing, flossing, and check-ups at Clinique Dentaire Pincourt are essential to maintain your implants and overall oral health. With the necessary care, your new smile can last a lifetime.
