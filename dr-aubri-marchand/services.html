Recall and preventive visits

Ideally a child should have a dental check-up and cleaning twice a year. During these regular visits, I will:

check the health of teeth, gums and mouth clean the teeth apply fluoride take xrays as needed

Sealants

This simple, non-invasive procedure helps to prevent cavities from forming in the grooves of molars. A protective coating is "painted and glued" into the grooves and crevices of back teeth.

Fillings (silver and white) and Crowns

Amalgam (silver) or Composite (white) fillings can be done depending on your preference. If a cavity is too large to fixed with a filling, a crown may be the best option for the longevity of the tooth. Silver crowns or “hats” are made with stainless steel and provide a high level of protection when cavities or big or when the risk of cavities are very high.

Silver Diamine Fluoride (SDF)

General Anesthesia

For children that are not be able to tolerate treatment awake or for those who require a lot of treatment, having dentistry performed asleep may be the best option. I offer this service at the Centre Dentaire Patrick Canonne with a specially trained pediatric anesthesiologist who will monitor your child throughout the procedure. Children are often feeling well by dinner time and can most often resume regular activities the following day.

Orthodontics

Dr. Aubri can evaluate your child’s need for orthodontic treatment. She can provide some early treatment options or refer you to one of many orthodontists she has worked closely with over her career.
