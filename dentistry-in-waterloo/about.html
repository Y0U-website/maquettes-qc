Dr. Holman Yu,

Dr. Holman Yu is a highly accomplished dentist with a rich educational background and a dedication to excellence in dental care. A graduate of the prestigious University of Connecticut School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Yu honed his skills in the United States for five years before returning to Canada in 2008 to prioritize family.

Read More

Before his advanced dental studies, Dr. Yu earned a Bachelor of Science with distinction from the University of Toronto, laying a strong foundation for his future success. His academic prowess and commitment to his community have earned him numerous accolades, including the esteemed Pierre Fouchard Award for future leadership and a community service award from the University of Connecticut.

Dr. Yu’s time in dental school was marked by significant extracurricular involvement. He played a key role in the Connecticut Dental Student Association, served as vice president of the American Student Dental Association (ASDA), and was a National Consultant on Diversity for the American Dental Association (ADA). Additionally, he distinguished himself as the chief editor of the University of Connecticut Dental Journal.

With a philosophy that blends scientific precision with artistic flair and heartfelt care, Dr. Yu has continually sought to enhance his expertise through ongoing education. His certifications in implants, SureSmile invisible braces, and nitrous oxide, ensure his patients benefit from a broad spectrum of advanced and personalized treatment options. Additionally, the use of cutting-edge technology such as Dentsply Sirona’s Primescan, PrimePrint 3D printer and SiroLaser in his office further enhances the quality and precision of the dental care he provides.

Discover why Dr. Yu is a trusted choice for dental care in Waterloo, Ontario by booking an appointment.

Appointments

We know you have many choices when choosing a dentist in Waterloo, so we have made requesting an appointment a simple process via our Web site. If, for any reason you cannot keep a scheduled appointment, or will be delayed, please call us as soon as possible.

Insurance and Billing

We are a fee for service office and payment is due upon completion of your appointment. Once your visit is completed, we will help you submit your insurance claim electronically. If you have direct deposit setup with your insurance carrier, then the reimbursement will get deposited into your bank account within a few days. Otherwise, reimbursement will come in the form of a cheque to your registered address. Forms of payment accepted are cash, VISA, Mastercard, and debit.

Cancellation

If you are unable to keep an appointment, we ask that you kindly provide us with at least 48 clinic business hours’ notice. A fee may be charged if a patient does not show up for an appointment without sufficient notice.
