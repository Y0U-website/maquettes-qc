What To Expect

Being well-prepared for your appointment will ensure that the doctor has all of the needed information to provide the best possible care for you. It also will help relieve any unnecessary anxiety you may be feeling.

Educate yourself on your symptoms by reviewing the content on this website, and bring notes to your first appointment so that you can discuss with Dr. Yu.

Your first appointment will include a full mouth series of x-rays, full mouth exam, treatment planning & charting, and oral cancer screening.

Also, take some time to review our staff page and familiarize yourself with the doctor and staff.
