Meet The Team

Behind every great clinic is a team of people who genuinely care. At Clinique Dentaire du Boulevard, our dentists bring together a wide range of specialties, backgrounds, and languages, united by one shared commitment: providing exceptional care to every patient, every visit.

Dr. Stéphanie Levi - Owner / General Dentist

A graduate of the Faculty of Dental Medicine at Paris VII University in France, Dr. Stéphanie Levi brings nearly 10 years of experience running her own private practice in the Paris region. Over that decade, she built a general dentistry practice grounded in one core principle: the trust and well-being of her patients.

Driven by a desire to broaden her horizons and grow in an international setting, she relocated with her family to Montreal in 2017. Her expertise was fully recognized by the Ordre des dentistes du Québec (ODQ).

In 2022, Dr. Levi reached a defining milestone in her Montreal career by becoming the owner dentist of Clinique Dentaire du Boulevard. Rooted in the vibrant Mile End neighbourhood, she has found her place in this dynamic, creative community, and it shows.

Warm, passionate, and deeply committed to her craft, she makes it her mission to deliver exceptional care to patients of all ages, with full transparency every step of the way. To stay at the forefront of modern dentistry, she actively pursues continuing education and advanced training to sharpen her clinical expertise.

My priority is for every patient to feel heard and completely safe in my care.

Dr. Eileen Margossian - General Dentist

A McGill University graduate, Dr. Eileen Margossian brings a high level of scientific and academic expertise to our clinic. With 14 years teaching oral surgery at the Université de Montréal, she has spent over a decade shaping the next generation of dental professionals while maintaining an active clinical practice of her own. The result is a dentist who performs even the most complex procedures with a level of precision and confidence that is immediately felt by her patients.

Her additional degree in microbiology sets her apart in a meaningful way. Where most clinicians meet the standard, Dr. Margossian goes further, ensuring that every aspect of care is delivered under the strictest conditions of asepsis and sterilization. For patients who value thoroughness and attention to detail, she offers a level of clinical rigour that is rarely found in a neighbourhood practice.

A true polyglot, Dr. Margossian welcomes you in French, English, Armenian, Arabic, or Italian. This remarkable range of languages reflects a broader cultural openness that patients notice the moment they walk through the door. No matter where you come from, you will always feel understood and respected in her care.

Outside the clinic, her passions for literature, music, and tennis give her a warmth and cultural depth that put even the most anxious patients at ease, turning what could be a stressful appointment into a calm, even pleasant experience.

Dr. Milagros Torres - General Dentist

With over 15 years of experience, Dr. Torres brings a well-established expertise in general dentistry, operative dentistry, and aesthetic restorations. Her clinical background spans a wide range of treatments, allowing her to provide comprehensive, personalized care to patients at every stage of life.

Known for her gentle chairside manner and remarkable patience, she has a natural gift with young children, turning their first dental visits into reassuring, positive experiences that set the tone for a lifetime of good oral health. Parents regularly trust her with their most anxious little ones, knowing they will leave the chair smiling.

A fluent Spanish speaker, Dr. Torres warmly welcomes patients in both French, English and Spanish, making the clinic an even more accessible and comfortable space for Montreal’s diverse communities. Clear communication is at the heart of her practice, and she takes the time to ensure every patient fully understands their treatment and feels confident in the care they receive.

Mindful of her patients’ busy schedules, Dr. Torres makes herself available a few Saturdays each month, offering the flexibility that families need to fit dental care into their lives without the stress. Whether you are coming in for a routine check-up or a more involved restoration, you can count on her to make the experience as smooth and comfortable as possible.

Dr. Mina Hanna - General Dentist (Practice Limited to Orthodontic Consultations)

A graduate of the Université de Montréal in April 2023, Dr. Mina Hanna stands out for his attentive listening and his drive to integrate the latest technological innovations into patient care. Passionate about the evolution of modern dentistry, he dedicates a significant part of his practice to continuing education — ensuring every treatment is delivered with optimal precision and quality.

While a general dentist by training, Dr. Hanna has chosen to focus his practice exclusively on orthodontic consultations here at Clinique Dentaire du Boulevard. With specialized training in clear aligners, he guides patients through two critical stages of dental development:

Phase I — Interceptive Orthodontics: Dr. Hanna works with younger patients to support and guide jaw growth. This preventive phase creates the space needed for teeth to develop properly, laying the foundation for a harmonious, healthy bite.

Phase II — Final Alignment: He corrects the positioning of permanent teeth to achieve a smile that is functional, aesthetic, and perfectly aligned.

Dr. Hanna is recognized for his professionalism and his genuine dedication to every patient in his care.

Dr. Antoine Butel - General Dentist (Practice Limited to Oral Surgery)

A graduate of the University of Lille in 2010, Dr. Antoine Butel brings a rich and varied career to our clinic. As a former co-owner of a dental practice in northern France, he successfully combined a demanding private practice with active involvement in the public sector — developing a broad, inclusive vision of dentistry along the way.

Drawn to the complexity of his field, Dr. Butel has dedicated a significant part of his career to mastering oral surgery. Always at the forefront of technological advancement, he incorporates digital innovations into his work to ensure precise, minimally invasive procedures and results that reflect the very best of modern dentistry.

Dr. Butel is known for his exceptional dedication to his patients. For him, every surgical procedure must take place in an environment of absolute comfort and complete safety. Beyond his clinical skills, what truly sets him apart is his deeply human approach to care, a quality his patients feel from the very first visit.

Dr. Angelina Evmenova - General Dentist

A graduate of the Doctor of Dental Medicine (DMD) program at the Université de Montréal (class of 2020-2025), Dr. Angelina Evmenova brings a rigorous academic foundation and a modern approach to dentistry to our clinic. From the very start of her practice, she has distinguished herself through a patient-centered philosophy, taking the time to listen, explain, and guide each person toward better long-term oral health.

She has a particular interest in restorative dentistry, orthodontics, and both therapeutic and aesthetic Botox treatments. This combination of skills allows her to offer complete solutions that balance function and aesthetics, all under one roof.

Committed to staying at the forefront of her field, she actively pursues continuing education to integrate the latest advances in modern dentistry into her daily practice. Warm and genuinely attentive, she creates an environment where patients feel at ease from the very first visit.
