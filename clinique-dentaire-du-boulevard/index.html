Le cabinet

Complete Care Our clinic covers the full spectrum of dental care, from routine cleanings to oral surgery, orthodontics, and cosmetic treatments. Our team brings together all the expertise you need, rarely found under one roof.

Over the years, the clinic has evolved with advances in dental technology, offering digital radiography, minimally invasive techniques, and personalized treatment planning. But what has never changed is our commitment to clear communication, ethical treatment recommendations, and respectful, attentive care.

Today, the clinic is owned and directed by Dr. Stephanie Levi , who continues the legacy of compassionate, high-quality dentistry while leading the practice into its next chapter of growth and innovation.

Our mission is simple: To provide modern, evidence-based dental care in a calm, welcoming environment where patients of all ages feel comfortable and confident.

At Clinique Dentaire du Boulevard, we believe that quality dental care should be accessible, comprehensive, and delivered by people you trust. That is why we have brought together a team of general dentists under one roof, so that whatever you or your family needs, the right expertise is always available right here at the clinic.

Whether you are coming in for the first time or have been with us for years, our approach never changes. We take the time to listen, explain your options clearly, and recommend only what is truly in your best interest. Your comfort and confidence are at the heart of everything we do.

Our fees are generaly based on the guidelines suggested by the ACDQ (Association of Dental Surgeons of Quebec) for the current year. The same guidelines are used by most insurance companies to assess dental claims.
