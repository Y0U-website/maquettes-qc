Dr. Ravi Patel

Dr. Ravi Patel is co-owner and clinical lead of Tooth Culture Dental (Waterloo) and Mount Forest Dental. An NDEB-certified clinician with a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (2012), Dr. Patel completed a one-year residency in implant dentistry at the University of Toronto. He is certified in nitrous oxide and oral sedation, allowing his team to deliver calm, evidence-based care for nervous and medically complex patients.

A dedicated educator, Dr. Patel has been actively teaching at the Dental Simulation Training Centre since 2016 and takes pride in mentoring the next generation of clinicians. He is also a member of the Ontario Dental Association (ODA). Dr. Patel’s philosophy is simple: combine technical excellence with compassionate communication so every patient can feel confident about their smile.

Outside the clinic, he cherishes time with his wife and children, explores new places through travel, and hones his strategic thinking over games of chess.
