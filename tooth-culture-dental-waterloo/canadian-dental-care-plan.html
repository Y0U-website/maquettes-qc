Eligible age groups include seniors aged 65 and over, children under 18, and adults with a valid Disability Tax Credit certificate.

Note: To date, all Eligible Canadians of all ages can now apply for the CDCP.

  • It uses income levels to decide how much of the cost it covers vs. what you must pay.
  • Some services require preauthorization before treatment to be eligible for coverage.
  • It does not cover all dental services. Cosmetic or elective treatments are often excluded.
  • Age and Residency : Be a Canadian resident for tax purposes.
  • Financial Status : Have an adjusted family net income lower than $90,000.
  • Insurance : Do not have private dental insurance.
  • Tax Status : Have filed a tax return for the previous year.

Benefits of the CDCP for Seniors The older adults aged 65 years and older will especially gain from the CDCP even if they lack any dental insurance for seniors, as it offers:

  • Preventive Care: Regular dental visits for check-ups, cleanings, fluoride treatments, and sealants.
  • Restorative Care : Fillings, crowns, and bridges.
  • Emergency Services : Dental care for immediate and major
  • Periodontal Care : Receive dental care for gum diseases.
  • Oral Surgery : Extractions and other surgical procedures.
  • Access to Desirable Procedures : Helpfully covered dental procedures that would otherwise be beyond one’s affordability.
  • Better Oral Health : Frequent checkups at the dentist in Waterloo ensure greater general well-being.
