CDCP is accepted at the clinic.

Since the federal government rolled out the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP), more than nine million eligible Canadians can receive covered dental care. Our clinic welcomes CDCP patients alongside everyone else — call us to confirm eligibility and schedule a visit.
