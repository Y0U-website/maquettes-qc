Family Dental Clinic with a Human Attitude

Family dental clinic serving the Park-Extension community for almost 30 years.

Le cabinet

We pride ourselves by being centred on you, the patient. We work with you to determine any illness you might have and work with you to come up with a strategy and a treatment plan that achieves your goals while staying within your budget.

We accept most forms of insurance; Payment cards; government coverage (bring your blue paper and medicare card with you); and federal interim refugee program (bring your ID paper with you), and the Canadian Dental Care Plan (bring your card with you).

Concerned by the inequities in access to care for people in poverty, Sofia got a MSc. degree in dental sciences at UdeM studying qualitative research and looking into community partnerships with dental faculties. Through UdeM as well, she directed the dental clinic at REZO . Sofia is also a Faculty Lecturer at McGill University. There, she works at the Jim Lund dental clinic at the Welcome Hall…

Sofia has a passion for languages. She’s fluent in English, French, Russian, Spanish, and Italian. On her spare time, she likes to work out at the gym and play the violin and classic guitar.

Nareg got his dental degree from Université de Montréal in 1996, and since then he has been working in the park-ex community at Centre Dentaire Jean Talon.

Concerned by the quality of oral healthcare of people living in poverty , he started a MSc. degree at McGill University studying qualitative research and developing a patient-centred approach in Dentistry. Over the years he published and continues to publish numerous articles on patient-centred dentistry .

Nareg has served for 6 years on the comité d’inspection professionelle of the Ordre des Dentistes du Québec. He chaired the committee the last 2 years of his mandate. Moreover, as assistant professor at McGill University for 5 years, he was director of the clinical activities of the 3rd and 4th year dental students.
