Scam Alert! Fake “Free Dental Implants” Ads Targeting Patients Online In recent months, dental practices across North America including reputable offices like Fairview Dental Center have seen a troubling rise in fraudulent online ads claiming to offer free dental implants, dental implant grants, or even payments for getting implants done. These ads often show up on popular platforms like Facebook, Instagram, and Google and are designed to look convincing, professional, and local.

What’s Really Happening These offers might seem like a dream come true, especially for patients struggling with dental health or the high cost of implants, but here’s the hard truth:

Some scams take it a step further by redirecting you to a generic landing page that looks official but isn’t linked to any real practice. And in more aggressive cases, scammers call patients using phone numbers of real dental offices to make their pitch seem authentic.
