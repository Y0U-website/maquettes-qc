CDCP

CDCP – Canadian Dental Care Plan We would like to thank you for your patience while we took the time to carefully review all the documentation regarding the government CDCP plan. We are pleased to announce that as of October 20th, 2024 we will be accepting this plan in our office.

While many dental procedures are subsidized by CDCP, this will not be at 100 %. It is important to note that patients will be responsible for the payment of any remaining balances. This is similar to individuals with private dental plans as the Quebec Order of Dentists ethics stipulate that all patients must be billed the same for all services regardless of insurance coverage.

As always, our priority is to provide you with the best possible dental care. If you have any questions or concerns about how the CDCP may affect your treatment options or costs, please don’t hesitate to contact us. Our team is here to assist you and ensure that you receive the care you need.

To access the benefits of the CDCP plan, patients must have a valid card from the government which they must apply online for. This card must be presented to us upon your arrival at the office. We are not able to help you with procuring the card.

The government places all participants in a particular category, yours may vary from those of your friends and neighbours.

Thank you for entrusting us with your dental health. We value your continued support and look forward to serving you.
