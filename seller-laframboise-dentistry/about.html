About

Our practice makes your smile perfect.

We believe that everyone deserves a healthy and happy smile. We also believe that dental care is important self-care. No matter your age, your oral hygiene is an important part of your whole-body wellness, and we’re committed to making dentist visits something you look forward to.

When you visit us, it’s like visiting a friend. Whether you’re here for a routine checkup or a restorative procedure , our goal is to ensure that you enjoy your visit with us. We care, we listen, we get to know the person behind the smile, and we always put your comfort first.

Dr. Mallory Laframboise Dr. Mallory Laframboise is our newest partner and an incredible asset to our team here at the clinic. She earned her DMD from McGill University, then completed her dental residency at The Hospital for Sick Children (at the University of Toronto) in 2020, bringing specialized experience in children’s dentistry . She has a room-brightening smile and a special knack for making all her patients feel comfortable, welcome, and always cared for.

Dr. Heather Brown Dr. Brown’s sunny attitude shines when helping her patients feel their absolute best. Also a graduate from McGill University, she has been perfecting smiles since 1991. Her emphasis on patient education leaves patients with a positive experience. In Dr. Brown’s spare time, she can be found outdoors with her kids and walking the family dog, Scruffy.

Dr. Sahar Khoshab Dr. Sahar graduated from McGill in 2015 and obtained her general practice residency at the Montreal General Hospital in 2016. With over ten years of experience as a dentist, she believes in making dental visits fun and provides a calm and reassuring environment for everyone. Her biggest joy is seeing her patients smile with confidence! Dr. Sahar recently moved to Ottawa with her family. In her free time, she enjoys exploring her new city with her husband and her baby daughter.

Dr. Daniel Seller

Dr. Seller has been creating healthy, beautiful smiles in the Ottawa area for over 25 years. After discovering his passion for sciences, he enrolled at McGill University and graduated with a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree in 1990. He loves finding the solution to any dental issue — from Invisalign to same-day CEREC crowns — and seeing it through until his patients are happy. When he is not improving Ottawa’s smiles, he can be found outdoors, staying active with his three daughters.
