Dr. Mancini

Dr. Mancini embodies the qualities of a genuine, compassionate, and professional dental practitioner. Her unwavering dedication to her patients and passion for the field of dentistry radiate through her interactions. With a commitment to staying at the forefront of advancements in dental care, Dr. Mancini takes pleasure in sharing her knowledge and devotes a portion of her time to educating others.

She holds a distinguished academic background, having earned her dental degree from McGill University’s Faculty of Dentistry in 1994. Following this, she embarked on a comprehensive multiresidency program at the Jewish General Hospital and further pursued postgraduate studies in periodontology, culminating in a Master of Science degree from the University of Toronto in 1998. Besides her thriving private practice specializing in periodontology and implant dentistry, Dr. Mancini actively contributes to the field as a clinical instructor in the Montreal General Hospital residency program.

Recognized for her expertise, Dr. Mancini has assumed leadership positions throughout her career. She has served as the president of the esteemed Montreal Dental Club and currently holds memberships in esteemed professional organizations, including the International Team of Implantology, the American Academy of Periodontology, and the Federation of the American College of Dentists.

Her contributions extend beyond clinical practice, as she has been invited to present lectures for the International Academy of Dental Research and continues to lecture extensively in the areas of periodontology and implantology.

In addition to her commitments, Dr. Mancini leads two Spear Study Clubs, renowned platforms that foster professional growth and knowledge sharing within the dental community. Her active involvement in these esteemed clubs showcases her dedication to collaborative learning and advancement in the dental profession.

Dr. Mancini’s outstanding qualities, comprehensive expertise, and continuous pursuit of excellence make her a trusted dental professional, delivering exceptional care and ensuring her patients receive the highest standard of treatment.
