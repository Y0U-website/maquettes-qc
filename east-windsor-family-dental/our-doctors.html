Meet Our Team

Dr. Laura Spinazze, D.D.S.

Dr Laura Spinazze was educated at Brennan Highschool, the University of Windsor and obtained her dental degree at the University of Western Ontario Schulich School of Dentistry. Her interests include hanging out with friends and caring for family. She is a fitness buff and is into photography.

Last but of course not least, Dentistry is her passion.

Dr. Jerri Ann Buxton, M.SC. D.D.S.

Born and raised in Kingsville, Ontario, Dr. Jerri Ann Buxton attended St. Anne’s Highschool, University of Detroit Mercy, Wayne State University and the University of Michigan School of Dentistry. She has worked in the Detroit area, Amherstburg and now East Windsor. She is the proud recipient of the United Way Volunteers Changing Lives Award for her dental work at Street Health. She continues to volunteer at the Alan Quesnal Community Dental Centre. A mother of four, she spends her free time running, doing yoga and traveling.

Samantha

Sam as everybody knows her, graduated from the Dental Assisting Program in 2006 and has had the pleasure of working in the field since 2007. She loves her job and loves helping people feel better about their smile. Sam got married in August of 2020 to her high school sweetheart. In her spare time she enjoys spending time on the soccer field or the ice rink with her 2 boys. She holds positions on two boards for her local community including soccer and hockey.

The outdoors is something that Sam appreciates, so on a nice day you will find her outside fishing, canoeing, biking or on the beach.

Marissa

Marissa graduated from St. Annes High School in 2016 . She graduated from the Dental Assisting Program at St. Clair College in 2019. She continues to extend her knowledge through the ODAA (Ontario Dental Assistants Association) by completing several courses. Marissa joined the team when she completed the program and has loved every second of it! During her free time, she enjoys hanging out with friends & family, walking and shopping.
