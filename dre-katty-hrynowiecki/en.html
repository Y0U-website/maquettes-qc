Behind the professional there is a curious person, attentive to others and to the world around her. Katty enjoys travelling, discovering other cultures and taking the time to observe.

That same attention runs through the way she practises: listening before proposing, explaining in plain language and looking for what genuinely suits each person.

Originally from Saguenay Lac-Saint-Jean, she studied dentistry in Montreal before settling in the Granby area, where she has practised since 2008, serving patients from Roxton Pond, Granby and the surrounding communities.

Her practice rests on listening, gentleness and clarity. She takes the time to assess each situation carefully, to explain the options in plain language and to accompany her patients calmly and respectfully.

Her interest in facial aesthetics follows the same measured, professional approach, guided by facial harmony, the patient’s comfort and respect for their expression.
