  • Preservation of Bone Health : Unlike other tooth replacement options, dental implants stimulate the jawbone through chewing forces, which helps prevent bone loss and preserves bone density over time. This preservation of bone health maintains the facial structure and prevents the sunken appearance that can occur with missing teeth.
  • Natural Appearance : Dental implants are crafted to closely mimic the function and appearance of a natural tooth so that it can blend seamlessly into your smile. The prosthetic tooth is custom-designed to match the surrounding teeth in colour, shape, and size, resulting in a natural-looking smile.
