Handpiece repair

We offer several handpiece maintenance & repair services, including:

Highspeed Handpiece Diagnostic and Tune-up Service We will test and give a free estimate on any handpiece, if no parts are needed we will deep clean, lubricate, align and retest each unit for $35.00.

Standard service :We replace bearings and O-rings, clean, lubricate, polish and test manual chuck handpieces and return the same day if called by 10 am. 6 months warranty.

We also offer Deluxe service on high speed dental handpieces. If you call by 10am, we will install a brand new balanced turbine, clean, lubricate, polish, test and return your handpiece by 5pm the same day. (Prices start at $179.00 for manual chuck handpieces)

We stock: Star Dental, Midwest, Kavo, NSK, MK-dent and W&H parts, including rotors, bearings, spindles, turbines, end caps, lamps, quickconnects, wrenches, gaskets, O-rings, washers, cleaners and lubricants.
