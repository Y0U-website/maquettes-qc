Dr. Israa Al-Ani

Primary Dentist

Dr. Israa Al-Ani is a young active dentist who graduated with distinction in 2014 from University of Sharjah. She acquired and developed her clinical skills through her experience as a clinical instructor and general dentist. Dr. Al-Ani has been efficiently serving the Waterloo Wellington region since 2017 and prides herself on being an active member of the RCDSO - Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario, ODA - Ontario Dental Association , CDA - Canadian Dental Association, and the WWDS - Waterloo Wellington Dental Society.

Dr Al-Ani is a highly motivated individual best described as gentle, passionate and empathetic dentist who is always eager to learn new technologies and develop new skills. She is excited to offer variety of services such as Invisalign, implants, oral cancer screening, sedation and known to enjoy working with kids and taking care of your dental needs in a friendly, welcoming environment.

On her spare time, she enjoys traveling and spending time with her husband, daughter and their cat.
