All-on-4 implants in Waterloo

Recent advancements in the dentistry field have made restoring a healthy and beautiful smile a lot easier. Before this, loss of teeth or extensive damage meant wearing uncomfortable dentures. So, when implants were developed, people were now able to replace their natural teeth with replacement teeth that functioned the same. The implants in Waterloo that have become popular are All-on-4 dental implants. Read on to learn more.

Also known as snap-in dentures, All-on-4 dental implants are made up of several vital elements. Four implants are placed into the jaw, which then becomes the anchor points for long-lasting and stable dentures. Apart from this, four abutments are placed above the gum where your replacement teeth will be mounted. This way, you will have a row of fully replaced teeth. Unlike removable dentures, these types of implants are known as semi-permanent replacements. If you manage them carefully, these implants will last a lifetime.

The All-on-4 dental implants procedure is done under anesthesia to make sure that you don’t feel any pain or discomfort. It is performed in two stages. In the first stage, your surgeon will prepare your jaw and mouth for the implants. During this time, they will also remove any broken or damaged teeth along with infected tissue. In the case of major infections, you might need medication.

The surgeon will start by embedding titanium screws into your jawbone in the second stage. These screws will act as anchors supporting your new dentures. Then, they will locate two implants towards the front and another two towards the back for stabilizing the dentures. The exact location depends on your natural bite and jaw shape. The anchors are located in a way that distributes pressure evenly across your jaw. After that, the dentist will clean your mouth and suture your wounds.

Getting All-on-4 implants can help improve your oral health. Here are a few key benefits of the procedure that you should know about.

All-on-4 implants offer a permanent solution. You won’t have to go through multiple fitting sessions. The denture is securely fastened to your mouth, which will give you greater convenience and comfort.

Patients with All-on-4 implants end up with a set of teeth that look natural. It will not only allow you to enjoy your favorite foods but also give you a self-confidence boost. You won’t be worrying about loose dentures and embarrassing yourself in public.

Taking care of your All-on-4 implants is easy. All you have to do is maintain your regular oral care routine, such as flossing, brushing, rinsing, and going for regular checkups. Just keeping up with these habits will ensure that you have healthy teeth for a long time.

Degradation of the bone is a common effect of missing and lost teeth. The All-on-4 implants can minimize the impact of bone loss. These implants are directly affixed to the jawbone and work similarly to the roots of natural teeth.

All-on-4 implants can help restore your facial features by stopping the resorption process. Because of the titanium rod in your jawbone, the body gets the signal to send calcium to your jaw. Therefore prevents the loss of bone matter.
