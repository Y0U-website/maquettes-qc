Ease of Payment

We offer direct billing to most private dental insurance companies including Manulife, Blue Cross, Sunlife Financial, Green Shield Canada (GSC), Desjardins, and Canada Life. Direct billing means you never have to submit receipts or complete any paperwork. It’s one less thing for you to worry about!

For treatment balances not covered by your insurance, we accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Interac debit, e-transfer, cash, and cheque.

Payment is due in full at the time of treatment.
