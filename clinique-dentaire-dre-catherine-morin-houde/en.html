Dr. Catherine Morin-Houde

Dr. Catherine Morin-Houde is pleased to offer you a spacious, comfortable dental clinic in the Domaine du Vieux-Port area. Our family-oriented clinic puts people first and delivers personalized care based on your unique needs. With a skilled and caring team, we aim to make ourselves trusted partners in caring for your wellbeing.

Our highly qualified team is known for being available, attentive and approachable. We’re also proud of our new space, which promotes relaxation and is equipped with state-of-the-art equipment for optimal patient care. Some of the technologies available on site include digital X-rays, intraoral cameras and ergonomic exam chairs with massage functions. In short, we aim to make you comfortable every step of the way, from the waiting room to the treatment room.

Come to us for a wide range of quality services, including general and cosmetic dentistry, implantology, prosthodontics, or treatments for gum disease, bruxism and more.

Dental emergency? Call us right away and we’ll fit you in as soon as possible.

A modern approach to dentistry

We offer high-quality services for the whole family, including general and cosmetic dentistry as well as implantology.

Dental services for the whole family

Our approach to family dentistry involves establishing a relationship with patients from a young age and meeting their needs at every stage of life. We firmly believe in building trust to facilitate communication and learning.

We’re proud to offer a child-friendly environment. Our team uses a personalized approach to introduce kids to dentistry and teach them good oral care habits.

A beautiful smile

A beautiful smile boosts confidence and self-esteem and reflects good overall health. There are many cosmetic dentistry options available to help you show off your best smile.

Dental Implants

Losing teeth can have a detrimental effect on your oral and overall health. Dental implants can replace missing teeth and help you stay healthy.
