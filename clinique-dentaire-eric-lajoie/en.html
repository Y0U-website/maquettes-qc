Advanced technologies that we use

CEREC

CEREC is a state-of-the-art German technology that makes it possible to manufacture a crown, inlay or facet at the clinic. You therefore avoid multiple appointments.

Advanced caries detection

Some cavities are difficult to detect with the usual methods. The laser of the DIAGNOdent device allows early diagnosis of cavities. Thus, we can repair the tooth before the damage becomes too advanced.

Oral cancer detection

During our dental exams, we screen for oral cancer. When this cancer is detected early, the chances of survival after 5 years are 80 %.

Precision scanning

With our new Primescan camera, taking digital scans of your teeth is quick and accurate. In addition, you do not have to suffer the use of alginate paste.

Intraoral camera

The intraoral camera is used to take pictures of the inside of the mouth and to show them to you. This helps you understand and visualize the health of your teeth and gums.

Canadian Dental Care Plan

The Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP) is a government dental insurance program that covers the costs of dental care for Canadians and permanent residents with modest incomes.

The CDCP provides coverage for essential dental care, such as exams, cleanings, x-rays, extractions, restorations and dental preparations, as well as preventive dental care, such as dental updates. caries and sensitivity treatments.

All age groups are now accepted.

This initiative aims to reduce dental health inequalities and ensure that everyone, from children to seniors, can benefit from preventive and corrective treatment. By facilitating access to care, the Canada Dental Plan helps improve the overall health and quality of life of Canadians.
