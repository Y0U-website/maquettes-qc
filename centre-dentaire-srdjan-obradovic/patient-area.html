FIRST VISIT

During your first visit, we will perform a complete intra-oral and extra-oral examination, taking any necessary x-rays to establish a diagnosis of your oral health. The dental hygienist will perform a cleaning and communicate oral hygiene practices tailored to your needs.

To speed up the process, we invite you to fill out the medical form before your appointment. Once completed and saved, please email it to us at: reception@centredentairenb.ca

If you prefer to print it, please bring it with you to your visit.

Centre Dentaire Nadia Bergham respects the Privacy Act and all information transmitted to us remains confidential.
