OUR DENTAL TECHNOLOGIES

Ever since he opened his dental clinic in 1982, Dr. Dussault has wanted to practice dentistry at the cutting edge of technology: paperless patient records, low-radiation digital radiography, computer-designed and computer-manufactured crowns, laser-based caries detection, oral cancer detection, 3D radiography for orthodontics and implantology, dental microscope, digital scanning , etc.

In addition to improving patient comfort, these technologies enable better diagnostics, precision work and a shorter visit to the treatment room. The technologies we use also make us more productive. That’s why our rates are competitive and aligned with the guide of the Association of Dentists of Quebe c.
