Implant supported crown vs dental bridge

After losing a tooth, the traditional solution was a fixed dental bridge . Although this solution is somewhat less expensive initially, it is not without problems:

  • Comfort, feel, and appearance of a natural tooth.
  • Stimulation of the jawbone and prevention of bone loss .
  • Lower long-term cost.
  • Preservation of adjacent teeth.
  • Same oral hygiene as a natural tooth.
  • A dental bridge lasts around 10 years, sometimes a little more. An implant supported crown is much more durable when properly cared.
  • With a dental implant, the jawbone continues to receive stimulation and maintains its volume. This is not the case with a bridge .
  • A bridge requires that adjacent healthy teeth be trimmed to receive the crowns which will serve as pillars to the bridge. Not only does this weaken the teeth, but they must now support additional chewing forces . Consequently, the risk of root canal and tooth fracture is greater .

To determine the solution that is best for your situation, consult your dentist
