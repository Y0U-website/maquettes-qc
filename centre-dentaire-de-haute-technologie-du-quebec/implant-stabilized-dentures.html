Do you need 2 or 4 implants?

The number of implants will depend on your anatomy and your goals. This must be discussed with your dentist.

Procedure

  • X-ray and initial consultation.
  • Selection and approval of treatment plan;
  • Bone graft (only if required).
  • Implant placement and temporary denture (if possible, we will temporarily use your denture).
  • Follow-up appointments.
  • Installation of the final dentures.
