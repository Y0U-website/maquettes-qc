Clinique Dentaire Joliette: Complete Dental Care in Montreal

Clinique Dentaire Joliette can see patients who are in pain immediately, 7 days a week, thanks to an on-site rotation system of 6 dentists. You don’t have to wait for an appointment we can see you the very same day. We perform general dental surgery for all tooth problems as well as extractions if needed, every day of the week.

Clinique Dentaire Joliette has been operating for almost 25 years, and is partnered with the adjacent Pharmaprix and the medical clinic. You will find 6 dental surgeons on site, who all belong to the Ordre des dentistes du Québec and are members of the Association des chirurgiens dentistes du Québec. We offer comprehensive oral treatments for the whole family and a fixed price is established by the Association des chirurgiens dentistes du Québec for each procedure.

We accept all insurance as well as the welfare assistance program.

The dental care offered at the clinic is for the whole family. We work in a medical complex that is open 7 days per week to better serve our clients and meet your needs.
