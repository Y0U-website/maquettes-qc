Meet Dr. Matthew Bagnulo

Dr. Bagnulo received his bachelor s degree in Biology from the University of Washington. He then went on to study at Columbia University, in New York City, where he received his Doctorate of Dental Surgery.

Dr. Bagnulo grew up in the Laurelhurst neighborhood in Seattle and graduated from Roosevelt High School. His love of the Pacific Northwest brought him home from the east coast upon graduation from dental school. Dr. Bagnulo enjoys getting to know his patients, who make working in a dental office interesting.
