About Us

Experienced, Comprehensive & Patient-Focused Dental Care

Our clinic brings together advanced general dentistry, implant rehabilitation, and restorative expertise to deliver high-quality care under one roof. Dr. Mohamed Youssef has over 20 years of experience in general dentistry and implant treatment, with advanced training in implant surgery, bone grafting, periodontal procedures, and complex restorative care.

Together with seasoned prosthodontist Dr. Michael Moscovitch, our team focuses on creating healthy, functional, and natural-looking smiles while ensuring every patient feels comfortable, informed, and cared for throughout their treatment journey.
