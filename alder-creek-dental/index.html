Missing teeth need to be replaced not just for appearance but also for your oral health. Empty spaces allow your remaining teeth to shift out of position. Also, empty sockets can lead to bone loss and serious facial and jaw issues.

Dental implants are among the best solutions for teeth replacement. They are fixed, secure, natural-appearing, and function just like organic teeth. At Alder Creek Dental, we have a skilled periodontist place dental implants in appropriate cases. Our team will take you through the process and answer all your questions.

Dr. Alder has the experience and training required to complete your dental implants . While dental implants are among the best answers to tooth loss, implants are basically just titanium posts. Implants need restorations to be complete, both functionally and cosmetically.

Dr. Alder and her team will help you choose the appropriate restoration for your situation. Then, they will provide you with natural-appearing restorations that restore your ability to smile, chew, speak, and laugh.
