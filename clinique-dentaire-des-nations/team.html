Dr Frédéric Bouchard

Dentist, owner

Dr Frédéric Bouchard is a 2014 graduate in dental medicine from Université Laval. He has practiced in Estrie since the very beginning of his career, and owns the Clinique des Nations since 2019.

Dr Bouchard holds a diploma from the Canadian Implant Institute, and is notably active in professional training. His passion for his work allows him to stay abreast of the lastest advances and techniques in dentistry. His competence and attention will definitely put you at ease.

He is a member of the Ordre des Dentistes du Québec, the Association des chirurgiens dentiste du Québec and the Société dentaire de l’Estrie.

Dr Félix Poirier-Steben

Dentiste

A Sherbrooke native, Dr Poirier-Steben has returned to the region and picked the Clinique des Nations to establish his practice. He holds a doctorate in dentistry from Université Laval, as well as studies in nutrition and kinesiology.

As a rigorous clinician, he is a clear and thorough advisor. Using the dynamic approach that distinguishes him, Dr Poirier-Steben will ensure your comfort and treat you to the highest standards of the profession.

Outside of dentistry, Dr Poirier-Steben is also a music aficionado and an avid weekend warrior.

Dre Laurianne Landry

Dentiste

Dr. Laurianne Landry obtained her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Université Laval. After practicing in Gaspésie&#x27;s Baie-des-Chaleurs, Dr. Landry has finally returned to her hometown of Sherbrooke.

Dedicated to her profession and having completed continuing education courses, particularly in the field of aesthetic restorations, she is committed to helping each of her patients regain their smile. With her gentle, reassuring, and caring nature, Dr. Landry knows how to put all patients at ease.
