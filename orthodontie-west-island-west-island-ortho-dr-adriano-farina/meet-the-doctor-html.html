MEET THE DOCTOR

DR. ADRIANO FARINA, D.M.D., M.SC., FRCD (C)

“Life is all about enjoying the wonderful moments it has to offer, and smiling is a crucial part of enjoying those moments. Being able to give people beautiful smiles that make them feel confident is the most rewarding part of my job.”

For Dr. Farina, the greatest part about practicing orthodontics is building relationships with all of our patients and their families. Every new beautiful smile we design is an opportunity to impact and change someone’s life. Our patients bring us so much joy, and seeing that final beautiful smile is an incredible source of energy and inspiration.

A proud and native Montrealer, Dr. Farina earned his dental degree at McGill University in 2011 and graduated at the top of his class with multiple awards. He then spent a year honing his skills as a fellow at the Montreal Children’s Hospital, specializing in treating children and special needs patients. He earned his specialty certificate in Orthodontics and his Masters of Science in 2014, from world renowned Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio, where he was awarded the Sanford Neuger Award for Clinical Excellence.

Upon returning to Montreal, he has joined the West Island Ortho team and is committed to using his energy and enthusiasm to provide the community with wonderful and healthy smiles.

In addition to providing orthodontic care to the communities in the West Island region of Montreal, Dr. Farina heads the orthodontic clinic at the Montreal Children’s Hospital. There he teaches some of the top residents in Canada, and oversees two clinics: one specializing in the treatment of young children, and one aimed at providing orthodontic care to children with special needs.

Dr. Farina is a proud member of the Order of Dentists of Québec (ODQ), the American Association of Orthodontists (AAO), and the Canadian Association of Orthodontics (CAO).

In his free time Dr. Farina enjoys playing hockey, soccer, cooking and travelling!
