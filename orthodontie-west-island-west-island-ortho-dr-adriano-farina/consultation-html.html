ORTHODONTIC CONSULTATION

NEW PATIENTS WELCOME! NO REFERRAL REQUIRED!

A consultation appointment typically last 45 minutes.

Dr. Farina will examine you or your child and explain if you are ready to begin your orthodontic treatment and what options are available to you. Photographs and any necessary x-rays will be taken to help explain your orthodontic problem. If you have any type of dental insurance please bring all pertinent information to your appointment (insurance carrier and policy number).
