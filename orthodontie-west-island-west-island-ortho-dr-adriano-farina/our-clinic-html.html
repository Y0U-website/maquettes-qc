THE WEST ISLAND ORTHO EXPERIENCE

At West Island Ortho not only are we committed to giving you the best smile possible, but we want to ensure you have an amazing time throughout the process. We pride ourselves on treating all our patients in an energetic, fun and caring environment!

PATIENT EXPERIENCE

Most importantly we realize that we treat people and not just their teeth. We understand that choosing an orthodontist is a big decision, which is why we emphasize creating a warm, relaxing and welcoming atmosphere. Not only do we strive to create fantastic smiles, but we pride ourselves on going the extra step to provide you with the best experience possible. Our goal is to ensure you enjoy every one of your visits with us. We have also expanded our office hours to better accommodate the demanding schedules of our patients and parents.

ADVANCED TECHNOLOGIES

Using the latest technologies and products, our skilled and dedicated staff can provide you with a healthy and beautiful smile that you can share with the rest of the world. We offer a wide variety of orthodontic treatments, including Invisalign, ceramic and conventional braces. To make our office more patient friendly we’ve incorporated a digital scanner, thus eliminating the need for messy and uncomfortable impressions.

CUSTOM OPTIONS

From children, to teens, to adults, we love seeing new patients! Because everyone is different, we believe each of our patients should receive an individualized treatment tailored to their specific orthodontic needs. Come visit us to learn how we can give you the smile you’ve always wanted and deserved. We’re excited to meet you!
