FAQs

1. How long does Invisalign treatment take?

Treatment time varies for each patient depending on the complexity of alignment. During your consultation, your smile is carefully assessed and a personalized timeline is outlined.

2. Is Invisalign comfortable?

Clear aligners are designed to guide teeth gradually and gently into alignment. Most patients find Invisalign comfortable and easy to adapt to.

3. How often do I wear my aligners?

For optimal results, aligners are worn throughout most of the day and removed only for eating, drinking, and oral hygiene.

4. How do I know if Invisalign is right for me?

The most effective way to determine if Invisalign clear aligners are right for you is through a consultation at Ilios Dentaire. During this visit, your smile is thoughtfully assessed, your goals are explored, and a tailored approach is recommended to support comfort, function, and long term oral health.
