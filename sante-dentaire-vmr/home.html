What is the price of a comprehensive dental diagnosis?

The cost of a comprehensive dental diagnosis can vary depending on several factors, such as the patient’s oral health condition, necessary examinations, and proposed treatments. We recommend scheduling an appointment with a dentist for a thorough examination and a personalized quote. Santé Dentaire VMR sets its prices following the fee guide recommended by the ACDQ for the current year.

Can treatments be covered by insurance?

Yes, in most cases, dental treatments can be covered by dental insurance. However, this depends on your insurance coverage. We recommend our patients to check with their insurance company to understand the details of their plan and specific coverages. At Santé Dentaire, we take care of submitting the claims for the treatments provided to your insurer to ensure you receive reimbursement as quickly as possible.

How do I know if the recommended dental care will be covered by insurance?

At Santé Dentaire VMR, we send an estimate of the treatments to your insurance before your appointment to give you an idea of the reimbursement you may receive. However, it’s important to note that our treatments are not based on insurance coverage but rather on the patient’s oral health.
