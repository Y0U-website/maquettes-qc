Serving St-Eustache since 1996

4 DENTISTS AND 6 CHAIRS TO SERVE YOU BETTER

Our clinic has grown to meet the demands of our growing clientele

We deliver a personalized and professional approach to dental care that includes prevention and restoration. We offer regular dental check-ups, procedures such as root canals and endodontics, and non-invasive techniques to save teeth.

Whatever your unique situation, we can simplify even the most complex procedures so you can make an informed decision as to what is the best treatment for your oral health. We believe that a beautiful smile is a healthy smile and we’re here to give you and your family the best possible oral care.

Since 1996, we’ve been at our current location in St-Eustache. Over the years, our clinic has grown to meet demand, and we now have 6 chairs and 4 dentists to serve you better. We are a family practice and we serve clients young and old. In many cases, we serve many generations of the same family. Don’t hesitate to book an appointment to see how we can help you!
