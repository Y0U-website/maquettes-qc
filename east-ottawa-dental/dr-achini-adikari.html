Get to Know Dr. Achini Adhikari

Ottawa Dentist

(613) 704-5420 Book Online 1193 St. Laurent Blvd Dr. Achini Adikari Family Dentist In East Ottawa Hi,

I’m Dr. Achini Adikari, and my path to dentistry has taken me across continents. I completed my undergraduate dental degree in Sri Lanka before pursuing and achieving my dental equivalency here in Canada, a journey that taught me resilience, empathy, and how much of a privilege it is to care for patients every day.

Dentistry gives me the opportunity to help people feel comfortable, confident, and truly taken care of, and that’s what I find most rewarding. There’s nothing quite like seeing someone leave the clinic feeling better than when they arrived..

When I’m not at the clinic, you’ll usually find me outdoors. I love hiking, canoeing, and discovering new places whenever I can. But no matter where I go, my favorite moments are the ones I spend with my children and family.

I’m grateful for the chance to be part of this community and to support my patients on their journey to better oral health. I look forward to meeting you soon.
