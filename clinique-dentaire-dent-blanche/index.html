Dr. Stephanie Le Dao graduated from the University of Montreal in 2007 where she received her Dental Medicine Doctorate. She won the “Force Avenir” award for her remarkable contributions to society. She then went on to complete a General Practice Residency at Rochester General Hospital in New York State.

She regularly attends conferences all over the world in order to keep up to date with the latest technologies and procedures. She has certifications for the CEREC technology and the Pinhole Surgical Technique. She also took orthodontics, occlusion and esthetic courses.
