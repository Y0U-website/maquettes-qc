Le cabinet

At Centre Dentaire St-Leonard, we are dedicated to providing our patients with the highest standard of dental care.

This means more than just good dental treatment in a friendly setting. It also means using the latest tools and the best techniques in a safe environment. Our team and our technology make patients comfortable, improve safety and diagnostics, and reduce appointment/treatment times. We are committed to making your experience positive and rewarding.

We are proud to offer the latest technology with intra oral camera and digital x-rays to reduce the risk of radiation exposure. Our in house laboratory provides quick servicing of bleaching kits, sports guards and night guards and orthodontics. All equipment and sterilizers are monitored and maintained daily to ensure that the standards of the Order of Dentists is kept.

Our awareness of the importance of sterility has provided our patients with excellent care for 17 years.

All our doctors are pleased to invite you into a serene environment where our friendly receptionists are waiting to greet you. They are ready to answer all your questions and concerns, and will schedule appointments according to your particular needs. Our knowledgeable dental assistants are recognized by their competence and pleasant chair-side manner.
