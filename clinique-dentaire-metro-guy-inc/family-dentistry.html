Family Dentistry

Periodic exams

Part of your routine dental care, along with having your teeth cleaned by the dental hygienist, includes having a complete dental exam performed by our dentists

Prevention

With good dental hygiene, you can greatly reduce your risk of getting cavities, gingivitis, periodontitis, and other dental problems

Ultrasonic Cleaning and Hygiene

State-of-the-art ultrasonic dental cleaning equipment cuts down on the time for scaling by removing more dental calculus at once

Restorative Dentistry

Modern restorations are designed to look and feel natural, improving appearance and protecting the teeth

Crowns and Bridges

Porcelain crowns and bridges will cover teeth, protecting while giving a seamless aesthetic appearance

Endodontics

Root canal procedure will save a tooth that was infected, damaged, or has pain related issues at the root

Dental extraction and wisdom teeth

Removal of the third molars or other teeth is recommended in cases of tooth crowding, pain or cavities

Sports mouth guards

Recommended for hockey, martial arts, skiing and other sports, reducing impact to the teeth in case of an accident

Bite plate - Bruxism

A bite plate or night guard is an effective solution to the effects of bruxism, absorbing griding and clenching, and easing mandibular tension
