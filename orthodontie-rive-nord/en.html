WHO WE ARE

WHAT IS ORTHODONTICS?

Orthodontics is a specialty in dentistry that deals with diagnosing, preventing and treating dental and jaw relationship irregularities. Correcting the position of teeth and bones to place them in the optimal relationship ensures long term health of your teeth, jaw bones and jaw joints. If your teeth aren’t aligned properly, you could develop tooth decay and cavities, bone loss, recession, gum problems, difficulty chewing, jaw joint pains and issues, and even speech impairment. Orthodontics can prevent and address these issues and give you a beautiful smile to live life confidently.

WHO WILL BENEFIT FROM ORTHODONTICS?

DENTIST? ORTHODONTIST? WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE?

An orthodontist is a dental specialist who is the best person to make the appropriate diagnosis of any dental and facial irregularities you may have and to prevent or treat these issues.

Orthodontists first become dentists, and then spend an additional intensive three years of university specialty training to obtain a Masters in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics.

For this reason, an orthodontists have a deep understanding of dental and facial skeletal growth issues, and the appropriate treatments and corrections to address the health issues resulting in the malocclusion and skeletal disharmony.

HOW LONG DOES ORTHODONTIC TREATMENT LAST?

Orthodontic treatment is undertaken to correct specific issues and improve the oral health of a person, with the added benefit of obtaining a beautiful smile, esthetic result and greatly improve a person’s confidence. The growth correction of the teeth and jaws is timed with the optimal growth of each person, which varies from individual to individual. Generally, orthodontic treatment takes about 18 to 24 months. The factors that influence the treatment time are growth of the mouth and face, a person’s cooperation during the treatment and the severity of the issues being corrected.

In certain instances, an orthodontist will recommend an orthodontic treatment that is short lasting during a child’s young age to address a very specific growth aspect to prevent more significant problems from developing and to maximize growth and long-term stability.

1st Day

The beginning of an adventure and the first step towards an amazing smile! With Invisalign, you will receive your active aligners and see how easy it is to place on your teeth. With braces, we will place the brackets and wires and you can have fun choosing colors. You may also be asked to wear rubber bands to correct the jaw relationship. We will share with you how to make your treatment comfortable and successful.

As you get use to them, you may feel small pressures on your teeth as they start moving, but this feeling will go away. Most of the discomfort is managed with Ibuprofen or Acetaminophen.

1st Week

After the first two weeks with your aligners, you will be ready to switch to the next aligner. You may find small little pressures as the movement continues to express and the aligner moves your teeth incrementally. You will have a great routine by now wearing your aligners almost all the time, taking them out only to eat or brush and floss your teeth.

After a week with your braces, you should be more and more comfortable with brushing and flossing. Most of the discomfort is managed with Ibuprofen or Acetaminophen.

Make sure you keep your teeth clean, brushing and flossing at least twice a day and rinsing with water the rest of the time. Remember to drink lots of water to prevent dry mouth.

1st Month

At this point, you’re used to your aligners or braces and you have become a brushing and flossing pro. You no longer experience discomfort and you are noticing some positive changes to the position of your teeth already!

You are ready to have the inconspicuous attachments added on your teeth to progress your orthodontic movements. You may also be asked to wear rubber bands to correct the jaw relationship.

Dr. Mandich expertly plans your orthodontic treatment to stage each aligner movement progression optimally and to help be easiest to manage with your work or school schedule. We will plan on seeing you back for your next appointment within a few months to continue the progress of your treatment.

1st Year

You will notice fantastic changes to your smile and be motivated by how your teeth are getting straighter and straighter, and your bite should start feeling much more comfortable when chewing.

Virtual Care We are here to help guide your treatment: send us pictures of your progress through the Invisalign App My care tab on a monthly basis. Dr. Mandich will give you feedback to ensure that your treatment progresses smoothly and stays on track.

2nd Year

Once all the major movements have completed and your bite is feeling comfortable, you are ready for detailing the final position of your teeth to Dr. Mandich’s professional custom prescription and expertise.

Dr. Mandich is proud of being at the forefront of creating personalized smiles and individual prescriptions with a very comfortable orthodontic experience.

Your treatment is almost done. It’s an exciting time and the most important phase, because these finishing touches are what make Dr. Mandich’s smiles so special. Dr. Mandich will take the time to look at all your face and the way each tooth centers itself in harmony so that you are happy with your smile.
