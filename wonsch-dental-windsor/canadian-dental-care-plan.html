Eligibility Criteria for CDCP

To qualify for the CDCP, you must meet certain requirements. These usually include:

  • Canadian citizenship or permanent residency: Applicants shall either be Canadian citizens or permanent residents.
  • Income limits: The household income should not be more than certain predetermined limits as per the system.
  • Priority to specific age groups: Children and senior citizens groups may have priority entitlement for some particular services.
  • No other dental coverage: Applicants should not be covered under any insurance or other benefits for dental services.
