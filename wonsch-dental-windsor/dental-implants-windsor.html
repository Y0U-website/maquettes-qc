Dental Implants in Windsor

  • Bone Loss Prevention Bone loss in the jaw often occurs when teeth are missing. Dental implants help stimulate the jawbone, which prevents bone loss from happening.
  • Improved Oral Function They restore the ability to chew and speak properly, which can be compromised by missing teeth.
  • Dental Bridges Support They can support dental bridges , eliminating the need to alter adjacent healthy teeth.

Benefits of Dental Implants

  • Natural Appearance The look and feel of dental implants matches natural teeth completely. The feeling that comes from a revitalized smile is confidence-boosting.
  • Durability They provide a long-term solution as the materials are sturdy and they’re locked in place. Proper oral hygiene is still required.
  • Improved Comfort Unlike removable dentures, implants are fixed in place, eliminating discomfort and inconvenience.
  • Enhanced Oral Health Implants do not require reducing other teeth, as a tooth-supported bridge does. This helps maintain the long-term health of your mouth.
  • Convenience Implants don’t require adhesives, so the worry of something like dentures slipping out of place isn’t an issue.

The Process of Receiving Dental Implants

  • Consultation and Planning Once you’ve booked your appointment with a dental clinic in Windsor that provides dental implants, the process begins with a comprehensive dental exam. A consultation follows to assess your oral health and determine if implants are suitable for you. This includes X-rays and possibly 3D imaging.
  • Implant Placement They are placed into the jawbone as a minor surgical procedure under local anesthesia.
  • Osseointegration Over the next few months, the implant integrates with the jawbone in a process called osseointegration. This creates a strong and stable base for the replacement tooth.
  • Abutment Placement After osseointegration takes place, the connected piece (abutment) is fitted to the top of the implant.
  • Crown Placement Finally, a custom-made crown is created in the look of your natural teeth matching the color, shape, and size. This crown is attached to the abutment.
