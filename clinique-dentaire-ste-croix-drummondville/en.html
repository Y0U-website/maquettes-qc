Welcome to the Clinique dentaire Ste-Croix Drummondville

Formerly known as Denis Raiche Dental Clinic, Dr. Khawam and Dr. Raiche, as well as the entire team at Ste Croix Drummondville Dental Clinic warmly welcome you.

We welcome you in a modern , dynamic and state-of-the-art environment . In order to provide a high-quality family dentistry , we offer a wide range of services. Dental examination, teeth and wisdom teeth extraction, partial and complete dentures, root canal treatment, crowns on implants and bleaching are among these.

We are also pleased to provide specialized care in maxillofacial surgery and implantology.

The Ste-Croix Dental Clinic is here for you. Having your oral health at heart, we can accommodate you in case of an emergency .

For convenience, free parking is available at all times.

Do not hesitate to contact us for more information or to plan your next visit.
