Dr. Reena Feider

General & Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Reena Feider loves seeing her patient’s beautiful smiles every day. It is a special reminder of why she chose to become a dentist. She finds dentistry to be a great way to help people return to health and allows her to use her creative skills to design flawless restorations. It is rewarding to see patients leave a dental office more confident, happier, and healthier.

Dr. Feider was born in Hamden, England, and raised in Montreal, Canada. She received her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Tufts University and her Doctorate of Dental Surgery from the University of Washington School of Dentistry in 2000. She then completed her General Practice Residency at the VA Medical Center in Long Beach, California.

After her residency, Dr. Feider returned to Seattle to become an associate in a general dentistry office and teach part-time in the Department of Restorative Dentistry at her alma mater.

She moved to San Diego in 2002 with her husband, so he could complete his oral surgery training at Balboa Naval Hospital. During her years in San Diego, Dr. Feider owned a dental practice for 12 years. After 13 years in San Diego, Dr. Feider, her husband, and twin daughters returned to the Northwest to be closer to family and friends. Outside of the office, she loves to spend time with her family, traveling, exercising, and fine dining.
