Dental Implants in Kirkland, WA

  • Implant-Supported Bridges: Implant-supported bridges should be considered for replacing multiple adjacent missing teeth. Dental implants provide the ability to anchor multiple teeth in a bridge. If you have several teeth missing in a row, then we may be able to use implants to secure a bridge in place.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures: For someone who is missing all their teeth, attachment-supported dentures provide a much more stable and comfortable solution compared to traditional denture solutions. The denture snaps onto the implants, which helps stabilize dentures much better than regular dentures.
  • Mini Dental Implants: Mini dental implants are smaller in diameter than regular implants and are a less invasive option for stabilizing loose dentures or for replacing small missing teeth in areas of limited bone. They are a good solution when normal dental implants cannot be inserted because of a lack of bone structure or space.
  • Same-Day Dental Implants (Immediate Load Implants): In selected cases, it is possible to place a dental implant and attach a temporary crown or bridge in the same appointment. You could come in with no teeth and leave with a new set of teeth the same day.
  • Single-Tooth Dental Implants: If you are missing just one tooth, a single dental implant will provide an independent replacement tooth. This is ideal if you only want to replace one tooth, without having to be concerned about the adjacent teeth.
  • Subperiosteal Dental Implants: These implants are not directly placed in the bone but instead placed under the gum line, above the bone, using a custom-fit metal frame with posts that support the prosthetic teeth. Because newer techniques have been developed, most dental implant procedures no longer use this option.
  • Zygomatic Dental Implants: Zygomatic implants are a special option for those who have severe upper jawbone loss. Zygomatic implants are longer and integrate into the zygomatic (cheek) bone. These implants are a special alternative for those who do not have sufficient jawbone for a standard implant.

Why Choose Dental Implants?

Dental implants near you offer many benefits over other ways to replace missing teeth:

  • They look and feel like your natural teeth
  • You can eat all the foods you love
  • They help keep your jawbone healthy and strong
  • They don’t slip or move around like dentures can
  • With good care, they can last for many years
  • You brush and floss them just like regular teeth
