What is Orthodontics?

Orthodontics is a field of dentistry focused on diagnosing, preventing, and treating dental and facial irregularities. Its main goal is to straighten teeth and align jaws properly. This can help improve how your teeth fit together, how you chew, and the overall look of your smile. Our team provides orthodontic solutions to help address these concerns.

Who Can Benefit from Orthodontic Care?

Many people can benefit from orthodontic care, including both children and adults. Common issues we can help address include crowded teeth, gaps between teeth, overbites (when upper front teeth overlap the lower teeth too much), underbites (when lower teeth extend too far forward), and crossbites (when upper teeth sit inside the lower teeth). If you have concerns about your bite or the alignment of your teeth, we encourage you to discuss them with us.

Our Orthodontic Treatment Options

We offer various orthodontic solutions to meet different needs and preferences. These options are designed to gently move teeth into their correct positions over time. Our team can discuss the benefits of each approach with you to help determine the most suitable path for your smile journey. We provide solutions such as traditional braces and clear aligners.

What to Expect During Your Orthodontic Journey

Your orthodontic journey typically begins with an initial consultation where we assess your dental health and discuss your smile goals. We then develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your specific needs. The active treatment phase involves regular appointments to monitor progress and make adjustments. Once your teeth are in their desired positions, a retention phase follows, often using retainers, to help maintain your new smile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best age for orthodontic treatment?

Orthodontic treatment can be effective at almost any age. While many people associate orthodontics with teenagers, adults can also achieve great results. We recommend an initial assessment for children around age seven to identify any potential issues early. For adults, it s never too late to consider improving your smile and bite.

How long does orthodontic treatment usually take?

The duration of orthodontic treatment varies for each person, depending on the complexity of their case and the chosen treatment method. Some treatments may take a few months, while others can last for a couple of years. We will provide you with an estimated timeline during your consultation.

Is orthodontic treatment painful?

It is common to experience some mild discomfort or soreness when you first get your orthodontic appliance or after adjustments. This typically subsides within a few days. We can offer advice on managing any discomfort, and our team is always available to address your concerns.

How do I care for my teeth during orthodontic treatment?

Maintaining excellent oral hygiene is crucial during orthodontic treatment. We will provide detailed instructions on how to properly brush and floss around your braces or clean your clear aligners. Regular dental check-ups and cleanings will also continue to be an important part of your care.
