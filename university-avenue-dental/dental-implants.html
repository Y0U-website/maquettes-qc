Understanding Dental Implants

Dental implants are a modern way to replace missing teeth, designed to look and feel like your natural ones. An implant itself is a small, biocompatible post, usually made of titanium, that our dentists place into your jawbone. This post acts like a new tooth root. Once it has fused with your bone, a connector piece called an abutment is attached, which then supports a custom-made crown. This complete structure provides a stable and lasting foundation for your new tooth.

Are Dental Implants Right for You?

Many people who are missing one or more teeth can be good candidates for dental implants. Ideal candidates typically have healthy gums and enough jawbone to support the implant. Good overall health is also important for successful healing. If you are looking for a stable and long-lasting way to replace missing teeth, implants might be a great option for you. The best way to determine if dental implants are suitable for your specific situation is to schedule a consultation with our team.

Caring for Your Dental Implants

Taking care of your new dental implants is straightforward and very similar to maintaining your natural teeth. Regular brushing and flossing are essential to keep your gums and the areas around your implants healthy. We also recommend scheduling routine dental check-ups and cleanings at University Avenue Dental. These visits allow us to monitor the health of your implants and ensure their long-term success. With consistent home care and professional support, your dental implants can provide a beautiful, functional smile for many years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and good oral hygiene, dental implants are designed to be a long-lasting solution for tooth replacement. Many patients enjoy their implants for decades, and often for a lifetime. Regular dental check-ups and cleanings are key to their longevity.

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Our team prioritizes your comfort during the dental implant process. We use local anesthesia to numb the area, so you should not feel any pain during the procedure itself. You might experience some mild discomfort or soreness in the days following, which can usually be managed with over-the-counter pain relief.

How do I care for my dental implants?

Caring for your dental implants is similar to caring for your natural teeth. It involves regular brushing twice a day, daily flossing, and using any other oral hygiene tools recommended by our team. Routine dental check-ups and professional cleanings are also important to maintain the health of your implants and surrounding gums.

What is the success rate of dental implants?

Dental implants have a very high success rate, often above 95 %, when placed in suitable candidates and cared for properly. Factors like good oral health, adequate bone density, and following post-procedure instructions contribute to successful outcomes. Our dentists will discuss your individual case during your consultation.

Can anyone get dental implants?

While dental implants are a great option for many people, they are not suitable for everyone. Ideal candidates typically have healthy gums, sufficient jawbone, and good overall health. Certain medical conditions or habits might affect eligibility. We will conduct a thorough assessment during your consultation to determine if dental implants are the right choice for you.
