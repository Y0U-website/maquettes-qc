Professional Dental Hygiene for a Healthy Smile

Regular dental hygiene appointments are essential for keeping your teeth and gums healthy. Our team at University Avenue Dental is here to help you maintain your oral health for years to come.

What is Professional Dental Hygiene?

Professional dental hygiene appointments, often called dental cleanings, are a key part of preventative dental care. During these visits, our hygienists carefully remove plaque and tartar buildup from your teeth. This process helps prevent common dental issues like cavities, gum disease, and bad breath, contributing to your overall oral health.

The Importance of Regular Hygiene Visits

Regular dental hygiene visits offer many benefits beyond just a clean feeling. They are crucial for preventing the accumulation of harmful bacteria that can lead to tooth decay and gum inflammation. Maintaining good oral health through these appointments also supports your general well-being, as issues in your mouth can sometimes affect other parts of your body. Our team helps you keep your smile healthy and bright.

What to Expect During Your Appointment

When you visit us for a dental hygiene appointment, we want you to feel comfortable and informed. The visit typically begins with a thorough examination of your teeth and gums to check for any concerns. Next, our hygienist will gently remove plaque and tartar from your tooth surfaces, both above and below the gum line. This is followed by polishing your teeth to remove surface stains and a careful flossing. Finally, you will receive personalized advice on how to best care for your teeth at home.

Our Gentle Approach to Your Comfort

Your comfort is a top priority during every visit to University Avenue Dental. We understand that some patients may feel anxious or have sensitive teeth. Our dental team uses a gentle approach and modern techniques to make your dental hygiene appointment as comfortable and positive as possible. Please let us know if you have any concerns, and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.

Maintaining Your Oral Health at Home

While professional cleanings are vital, your daily at-home care plays a significant role in maintaining your oral health between visits. We encourage you to brush your teeth twice a day for two minutes each time, using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste. Daily flossing is also important to remove food particles and plaque from between your teeth and under the gum line. Consistent home care complements your professional cleanings, helping to keep your smile healthy.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have a dental cleaning?

Most patients benefit from a professional dental cleaning every six months. However, your dentist or hygienist may recommend a different schedule based on your individual oral health needs.

Does a dental cleaning hurt?

Dental cleanings are generally not painful. You might experience some pressure or mild sensitivity, especially if you have areas with significant plaque or tartar buildup. Our team works gently to ensure your comfort.

What is the difference between plaque and tartar?

Plaque is a soft, sticky film of bacteria that constantly forms on your teeth. If plaque is not removed regularly through brushing and flossing, it can harden into tartar, also known as calculus. Tartar can only be removed by a dental professional.

Can dental hygiene prevent gum disease?

Yes, regular dental hygiene is crucial for preventing gum disease, also known as periodontal disease. By removing plaque and tartar, professional cleanings help reduce inflammation and keep your gums healthy, preventing the progression of gum disease.

What can I do at home to keep my teeth clean?

To maintain your oral health between visits, brush your teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste and floss once a day. Eating a balanced diet and limiting sugary snacks and drinks also helps protect your teeth.
