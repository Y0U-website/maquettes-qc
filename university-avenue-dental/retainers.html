Dental Retainers

After completing orthodontic treatment, retainers are a crucial step to keep your teeth in their new, corrected positions. They help preserve your smile for years to come.

What Are Dental Retainers?

Dental retainers are custom-made devices designed to help maintain the alignment of your teeth after orthodontic treatment. Once braces or clear aligners have moved your teeth into their desired positions, a retainer prevents them from gradually shifting back. Wearing a retainer as instructed is a critical part of ensuring the long-term success of your orthodontic journey.

Types of Retainers We Offer

We offer different types of retainers to suit various needs and preferences. Removable retainers, like clear plastic (often called Essix retainers) or traditional Hawley retainers with a wire, are worn for specific periods each day. Fixed retainers, on the other hand, are thin wires bonded to the back of your teeth, offering a permanent solution. Your dentist will discuss the benefits of each type and help you choose the best option for your smile.

Who May Need a Retainer

Anyone who has completed orthodontic treatment, whether with braces or clear aligners, will likely need a retainer. Retainers are essential for maintaining the results achieved through orthodontic work. They help ensure that your teeth remain in their corrected positions, preventing relapse and preserving your aligned bite and smile for many years.

What to Expect When Getting Your Retainer

The process of getting a retainer is straightforward. First, we will take precise impressions or digital scans of your newly aligned teeth. These models are then used to create your custom retainer. Once your retainer is ready, you will return for a fitting appointment where we ensure it fits comfortably and provide you with detailed instructions on how to wear and care for it.

Caring for Your Retainer

Proper care is vital for keeping your retainer effective and hygienic. Removable retainers should be cleaned daily with a soft toothbrush and mild soap or a specialized retainer cleaner. Always store your removable retainer in its case when not wearing it to protect it from damage and loss. For fixed retainers, regular brushing and flossing are important to keep the area clean. Your dental team will provide specific care instructions tailored to your type of retainer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do I need to wear my retainer?

The duration of retainer wear varies for each person, depending on their individual orthodontic treatment. Initially, you may need to wear it almost full-time, gradually transitioning to nighttime wear. Your dentist will provide a personalized schedule for you.

What happens if I don t wear my retainer?

If you do not wear your retainer as instructed, your teeth may gradually shift back towards their original positions. This is known as orthodontic relapse and can compromise the results of your treatment.

How do I clean my removable retainer?

You should clean your removable retainer daily. Use a soft toothbrush and mild soap or a non-abrasive denture cleaner. Avoid hot water, as it can warp the plastic. Always rinse it thoroughly before placing it back in your mouth.

Can I eat or drink with my removable retainer in?

It is generally recommended to remove your clear plastic or Hawley retainer before eating or drinking anything other than plain water. Food particles can get trapped, and sugary or acidic drinks can damage your teeth or the retainer itself.

What should I do if my retainer breaks or gets lost?

If your retainer breaks, becomes damaged, or is lost, please contact University Avenue Dental as soon as possible. It is important to address this quickly to prevent your teeth from shifting. We can assess the situation and arrange for a replacement.
