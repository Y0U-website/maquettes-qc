Comfort and Care During Treatment

Invisalign aligners are made from a smooth, comfortable plastic material, designed to fit snugly over your teeth. While you might feel a slight pressure when you switch to a new set of aligners, this is a normal sign that your teeth are gently moving. This sensation typically fades within a day or two.

Proper care of your aligners is essential for a successful outcome. We recommend cleaning them regularly with a soft toothbrush and water, and always removing them before eating or drinking anything other than water. Wearing your aligners for the recommended 20-22 hours per day is crucial for achieving the best possible results. Our team will provide you with all the necessary instructions to ensure your treatment progresses smoothly and comfortably.

Maintaining Your Smile After Treatment

Once your Invisalign treatment is complete and your teeth have reached their desired positions, the next important step is maintaining your beautiful new smile. To prevent your teeth from gradually shifting back, which can happen naturally over time, our dentist will recommend wearing a retainer.

Retainers are custom-made devices, often clear and removable, designed to hold your teeth in their new alignment. We will provide you with specific instructions on how often and for how long you should wear your retainer. Following these guidelines is key to preserving the results of your Invisalign treatment and ensuring your smile stays straight for many years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Invisalign treatment usually take?

The duration of Invisalign treatment varies for each person, depending on the complexity of their orthodontic needs. On average, treatment can range from 12 to 18 months, but some cases may be shorter or longer. Our dentist will provide a more precise estimate during your consultation after assessing your specific situation.

Is Invisalign treatment painful?

Most patients find Invisalign to be a comfortable treatment option. You might experience some mild pressure or discomfort for a day or two when you switch to a new set of aligners, as your teeth begin to move. This sensation is normal and indicates the aligners are working. It typically subsides quickly.

How much does Invisalign cost?

The cost of Invisalign treatment depends on several factors, including the complexity of your case and the length of treatment required. We will discuss the full cost during your initial consultation after a thorough assessment. We also offer various payment options and can help you understand your insurance coverage to make treatment accessible.

Can I eat anything I want with Invisalign?

Yes, one of the great advantages of Invisalign is that you can remove your aligners for meals. This means there are no dietary restrictions, and you can continue to enjoy all your favourite foods. Just remember to brush your teeth before putting your aligners back in to maintain good oral hygiene.

How do I clean my Invisalign aligners?

Cleaning your Invisalign aligners is simple. We recommend rinsing them with water and brushing them gently with a soft toothbrush each time you brush your teeth. You can also use special Invisalign cleaning crystals or a denture cleaner as directed by our team. Avoid using hot water, as it can warp the plastic.
