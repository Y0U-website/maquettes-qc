Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Originally from Dundas, Ontario, Dr. Decloux enjoys going to the driving range with his sons, watching F1 racing with his wife, and taking a mid-day snooze. He loves playing soccer and building Legos with his sons, and you can find him golfing, cooking and entertaining, and coaching soccer and hockey in his free time!
