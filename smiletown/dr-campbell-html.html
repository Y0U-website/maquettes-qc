Outside of the Dental Office, What Do You Like to Do?

Whenever Dr. Campbell isn’t at the dental office, he loves spending time with his wife and daughter. Together, they’re always looking for the next adventure and a delicious meal! You can also find him woodworking, travelling and enjoying all of the delicious foods each location has to offer, skiing and snowboarding, and fishing.

An interesting fact about Dr. Campbell is that he used to be a garbage collector, and then an arborist, while he was in university!
