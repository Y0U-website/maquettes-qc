At SmileTown: Just Kids Dentistry, we’re proud to be insurance-friendly and try to ease the financial stress parents may experience trying to access dental care for their kids.

Dr. Adams is a part-owner at SmileTown, and he’s proud to be a part of such an amazing group of people. His favorite part of his job is working with kids he loves their energy and curiosity, and he finds the way they view the world inspiring. He also loves having a fun time, and kids make every day at the office exciting! You can learn more about Dr. Adams by reading below.

Dr. Adams was an undergraduate student studying health sciences when he realized that dentistry was a perfect blend of art and science. Anaesthesia has allowed him to take his career in a direction that he could have only dreamt of when he was young. He loves the opportunity to blend dentistry and anaesthesia into a career that he finds intellectually stimulating and rewarding.

Dr. Adams completed his undergraduate studies at McMaster (Hamilton) and earned his Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from Western University in London, Ontario. He went on to pursue a master s degree and complete a dental anesthesiology residency at the University of Toronto.
