Partial Dentures in Pointe-Claire: Where Comfort and Aesthetics are Essential

Partial dentures are an ideal solution for patients who still have natural teeth, but have lost some of them. They restore chewing function and prevent the remaining teeth from shifting, which is essential for maintaining good oral balance.

Types of Partial Dentures.

This option is often used as the quickest and least expensive solution. This type of prosthesis is used for one tooth commonly called a flipper or to restore several missing teeth. In addition, these denturescan be modified if a natural tooth needs to be removed, thus avoiding the cost of a new prosthesis.

More comfortable and durable, this option uses a framework with materials such as chrome/cobalt, Vitallium or titanium. Thanks to its thinner design and better adaptation to oral structures, it offers superior comfort to acrylic dentures. It also offers a better tactile sensation between hot and cold foods and for some patients the impression of better perceiving the taste of food.

For those who need a quick fix after extractions, immediate dentures can be made in advance and placed immediately after surgery. This ensures that you are not left without teeth during the healing period.

Why Choose a Partial Denture?

A partial denture replaces missing teeth while using the remaining natural teeth. This helps to preserve their position and avoid alignment problems. Molars and premolars play a crucial role in chewing and also provide protection for the front teeth. Every tooth counts, whether it is visible or not.
