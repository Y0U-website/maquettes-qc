Meet Our Friendly & Caring General Dentists In Ottawa!

Dr. Jean-Pierre Khouri

Born and raised in Ottawa, Dr. Jean-Pierre Khouri earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Université de Montréal in 2011. He has been proudly serving patients for more than a decade with precision, knowledge, and skill.

Fluent in French, English, and Arabic, Dr. Khouri easily connects with patients from diverse backgrounds. Known for his incredible work ethic, keen attention to detail, and heartfelt drive for helping others thrive, he provides care that’s effective, empathetic, and customized to each person in his chair. While Dr. Khouri is skilled in all aspects of general dentistry, he is particularly passionate about helping patients achieve healthy and confident smiles through orthodontic treatment and oral surgery.

Dr. Khouri is a member of the Canadian Dental Association, the Ontario Dental Association, the American Academy of Clear Aligners, the Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario, the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, and the Academy of GP Orthodontics. He is an active supporter of the Ottawa East community through causes that are close to his heart, including the Children’s Hospital of Eastern Ontario, SickKids® Foundation, and annual volunteer work with Bladder Cancer Canada.

Dr. Brianna St George

Dr. Brianna St George earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Universite de Montreal in 2019 before completing a General Practice Residency with McGill University, where she was named Chief Resident. She then practiced for 2 years in Whitehorse, Yukon before returning to her hometown of Ottawa. She has been practicing in our community for the past few years, delivering customized dental care in both French and English.

Dr. St George understands that dental experiences can be difficult for many and was inspired to pursue dentistry after experiencing dental trauma as a child. She focuses on putting patients at ease, restoring oral health and function, and elevating her patients’ confidence through personalized services. Recognizing that oral health is closely tied to overall well-being, she is dedicated to delivering care that fosters long-term wellness, confidence, and an improved quality of life.
