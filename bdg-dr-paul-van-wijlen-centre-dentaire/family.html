Did you know your child is covered for dental care?

Start your child at 2 years old!

Did you know that children can start coming to the dentist as of two years of age? By the time toddlers reach the age of two they’ve often already developed the majority of their primary teeth (also known as “baby teeth”). There are a total of twenty primary teeth, ten on the upper and lower arches, which begin to grow when your child is around six months old. Coming in for regular dental cleanings and exams allows them to get accustomed to the hygienist and dentist and teaches them the importance of their oral health.
