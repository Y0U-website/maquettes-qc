Dr. Gardner- Retirement

After many fulfilling years of practicing dentistry, I have made the heartfelt decision to retire. My last day at the clinic will be on March 31st 2026.

Le cabinet

This profession has given me the privilege of building lifelong relationships, caring for generations of families, and being part of your lives in meaningful ways. For that, I am truly grateful. The trust you have placed in me over the years has meant more than I can express, and I will sincerely cherish the memories and connections we have built together.

I am very pleased to share that the clinic will continue under the new name W Dental, led by Dr. Faranak Hashemi. Dr. Hashemi brings experience, dedication, and a forward-thinking approach to dentistry, including expanded services and modern techniques, while maintaining the same commitment to quality personalized patient care that you have always known.

I would also like to reassure you that Dr. Tarantino will continue her activities at the clinic and will remain available to care for her patients, ensuring continuity and familiarity in your dental treatment.

Stepping away is bittersweet, but I do so with complete confidence knowing that you are in excellent hands. Thank you for your loyalty, your trust, and the opportunity to serve as your dentist.
