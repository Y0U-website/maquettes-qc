How to Find Our Clinic

Our clinic is situated in the vibrant heart of Québec City, offering a central and easy-to-reach location for all our patients. You will find us conveniently close to the historic Gare du Palais train station, a well-known landmark that makes our clinic simple to spot. We are easily accessible from various parts of the city, whether you are coming from downtown, the surrounding neighbourhoods, or even visiting from out of town.

When you plan your visit, our office is located at #RC06 - 450 Gare de Palais St. We are nestled within the charming and bustling Old Port district, an area known for its rich history and welcoming atmosphere. This central location makes us a convenient choice for many patients seeking quality dental care in a pleasant environment.

Information for New Patients

We warmly welcome new patients to Clinique Dentaire Centre-Ville and are delighted you are considering us for your oral health needs. As a general dental practice, our dedicated team is ready to provide comprehensive and compassionate care for patients of all ages. We believe in building lasting relationships based on trust and clear communication, ensuring you feel comfortable and informed from your very first visit.

To help us make your initial experience as smooth and efficient as possible, we kindly ask that you arrive a few minutes early for your scheduled appointment. This allows ample time to complete any necessary new patient forms, which help us understand your medical and dental history. It is also helpful to bring your identification, current dental insurance information, and any relevant dental records or X-rays from previous clinics if you have them. Having this information on hand allows our team to provide the most tailored and effective care right from the start.
