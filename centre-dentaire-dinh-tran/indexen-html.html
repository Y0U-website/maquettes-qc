OFFERED SERVICES

Gum Treatment

Early detection of gum disease and effective treatment (disinfection care ) is recognized to help you keep your teeth.

Biteplate

Custom made device to prevent damage caused by clenching or grinding of teeth. It also helps to reduce pain in the jaw muscles and joint.

Cerec

We are able to replace your old fillings or make your ceramic crowns in one visit here in the clinic! No more embarrassing fingerprints and temporary crowns between visits !

Aesthetics ( Facet )

The facet can change the shape or color of one or more teeth. There are composite veneers which are less expensive but they are less durable . Porcelain veneers are therefore more useful because they are much more attractive and above all sustainable.

Denturist

Our denturist has over 10 years of experience . Also, on Monday and Wednesday , consultations fees are free.

Whitening

The ZOOM teeth whitening is safe and effective can be done in one visit to the clinic. It is essential to know that only your dentist or hygienist can advise you properly about the technique to use and when to do this treatment to avoid adverse effects and unnecessary pain .

Mouthguard

Comfortable and durable machine , custom-made to protect teeth against injuries in various sports such as hockey, football, soccer etc and also prevent or reduce the consequences of concussions .
