Dr. Marie-Reine Nader, D.M.D.

Dr. Marie-Reine Nader is a dentist with a passion for working with children and helping them develop healthy, positive relationships with dental care.

Since earning her dental degree in 2008, Dr. Nader has pursued extensive additional training in pediatric dentistry. Her experience includes caring for young children, adolescents, and patients with special healthcare needs, with a particular interest in creating a calm and supportive environment for children who may feel anxious about dental visits.

Dr. Nader believes that exceptional dental care for children extends far beyond basic oral health. Her primary focus is establishing a meaningful bond with her young patients. She customizes her approach to meet each child’s individual needs, ensuring they understand and feel comfortable with every step of their treatment. Her ultimate goal is to instill a sense of pride and lasting confidence in every child she treats.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. Nader is a mother of three, which has given her an even deeper appreciation for the importance of patience, empathy, and communication when caring for children.

She is delighted to be joining Dr. Schneidman and looks forward to welcoming children and families to the practice.
