Our Dentists at Dr. Wisdom VSL

Dr. Ahmad Wisdom Aoude

General Dentist Raised in a family of dentists, Dr. Ahmad Aoude earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the Université de Montréal. He has completed additional training in areas such as conscious sedation, orthodontic treatment including Invisalign, implant dentistry, endodontics, periodontics, and prosthetic rehabilitation.

Dr. Aoude regularly participates in continuing education programs and dental conferences in Canada and abroad to stay current with evolving techniques and technologies. He is a member of the Ordre des dentistes du Québec (ODQ), the Association des chirurgiens dentistes du Québec (ACDQ), and the Canadian Dental Association (CDA).

With many years of clinical experience, Dr. Aoude’s practice includes a strong focus on Invisalign orthodontic treatment, porcelain veneers, and dental implant care, always guided by patient needs, informed consent, and personalized treatment planning.

Dr. Rita Mouawi

Dr. Rita Mouawi graduated from Saint Joseph University in Beirut in 2012 and obtained BNED equivalency recognized in 2025. She brings over a decade of clinical experience and provides a wide range of general dental services, including preventive care, restorative treatments, and endodontic procedures.

Known for her gentle and patient-centred approach, Dr. Mouawi cares for patients of all ages and emphasizes clear communication and comfort throughout treatment. She is licensed by the ODQ and is a member of the ACDQ and CDA. She actively participates in continuing education to maintain current standards of care.

Dr. George Kesserwany

A graduate of the Université de Montréal, Dr. George Kesserwany began his career participating in a humanitarian dental mission serving underserved communities, an experience that continues to shape his patient-focused approach to care.

His academic and clinical work has been recognized through several distinctions, including the Micheline-Blain Award (ACDQ), the Quintessence Award for Research Achievement, and the Vice-Dean’s Award for Clinical Affairs in Dental Medicine. Dr. Kesserwany is dedicated to providing attentive, evidence-based dental care in a welcoming environment.

Dr. Ilyes Amrani, D.M.D.

Dr. Ilyes Amrani earned his dental degree from the Université de Montréal, following earlier studies in biomedical sciences. He remains actively engaged in continuing education and is a member of the ODQ, ACDQ, and CDA.

His clinical practice includes general dentistry services such as endodontic care, preventive treatments, restorative dentistry, cosmetic procedures, and wisdom tooth extractions. Dr. Amrani prioritizes patient comfort, clear explanations, and individualized treatment at every visit.

Marlo Sciarretta

Marlo Sciarretta is a licensed denturologist who provides complete, partial, and implant-supported dentures. With years of hands-on experience, Marlo focuses on creating comfortable, functional, and customized prosthetic solutions.

Patients appreciate Marlo’s clear explanations, attention to detail, and supportive approach throughout the denture process, from initial consultation to final fitting.

All members of the Dr. Wisdom team share a commitment to personalized care, modern dental techniques, and creating a comfortable experience for every patient.
