Dr George Kesserwany

General Dentist

Dr. George Kesserwany graduated from the University of Montreal and began his career by participating in a humanitarian mission with his team, providing dental care to over 600 patients in underserved communities. This experience reinforced his dedication to delivering comprehensive and patient-centered dental care.

Dr. Kesserwany has received the Micheline-Blain Award from the Association des chirurgiens dentistes du Québec (ACDQ), the Quintessence Award for Research Achievement, and the Vice-Dean’s Award for Clinical Affairs in Dental Medicine from the University of Montreal. These recognitions reflect his commitment to ongoing professional development and the well-being of his patients.

Outside of his practice, Dr. Kesserwany enjoys traveling, which allows him to explore different cultures and broaden his perspectives.

Dr. Kesserwany prioritizes the comfort and needs of his patients, ensuring attentive and quality dental care.
