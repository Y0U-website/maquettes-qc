Totem Lake Preventative Dentistry Services

Brushing and flossing twice a day are the best ways to help ensure the health of your teeth, but they do not eliminate all the plaque buildup that can occur. Our Totem Lake preventative dentistry practice offers quality care designed to clean and protect your gums and teeth from decay and other health problems.

Prophylaxis

This effective dental procedure thoroughly cleans the teeth and gums and stops the progression of gingivitis and periodontal disease.

Periodontal Maintenance

This is an effective procedure to prevent and prevent periodontal disease from starting or returning to your mouth. It is a routine cleaning with particular attention to tartar, bacterial, and plaque buildup above the gum line.

Root Planning

This effective treatment for those diagnosed with gum disease offers a precise deep cleaning that targets the gum line and tooth roots. The dentist will smooth out the surface of the root, making it harder for bacteria, plaque, or tarter to take hold and cause future damage.
