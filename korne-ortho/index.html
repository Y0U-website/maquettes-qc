Le cabinet

It is our goal to consistently exceed your expectations with regard to treatment and customer service. The blending of your expectations and our orthodontic expertise is of primary importance. Our aim is to create beautiful, healthy, properly aligned teeth so our patients can enjoy the physical and psychological benefits gained from smiles that look their best and function properly for a lifetime.

Our success is based on the relationships we enjoy with our patient families. We welcome you to our growing practice, convenient to downtown Montreal.

We utilize the latest technological advances in the industry, such as invisible ceramic and Spark® aligners along with the latest in computer technology (digital imaging, advanced computer graphics and the Dexis® intra-oral digital scanner) to ensure that you receive the most up-to-date care.

Whether you’re an adult, adolescent or child, our knowledgeable doctor and staff are committed to helping you achieve the smile you deserve – a healthy, beautiful one!
