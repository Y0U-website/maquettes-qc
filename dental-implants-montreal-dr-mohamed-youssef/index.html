Le cabinet

At our modern Westmount clinic, we provide personalized treatment plans using advanced technology such as 3D CT imaging, digital implant planning, and minimally invasive surgical techniques to ensure precise implant placement and long-term results.

Dr. Mohamed Youssef is an experienced implant dentist providing surgical and restorative implant treatment in Montreal.

He completed his Doctor of Dental Medicine at McGill University and later earned a Master of Science in Oral Implantology from Goethe University Frankfurt, one of the most respected implantology programs in the world.

Dr. Youssef is also a Diplomate of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (ICOI) and focuses on providing predictable, evidence-based implant solutions for patients who have lost teeth.

Our clinic provides a comprehensive range of dental implant procedures including:

• Single tooth dental implants • Multiple dental implants • Full mouth dental implants (All-on-4 / All-on-X) • Teeth in a Day dental implants • Immediate dental implants • Ceramic dental implants • Bone grafting and sinus lifts • Gum grafting and periodontal surgery

Dental implants are considered the gold standard for replacing missing teeth, restoring chewing function and providing a natural-looking smile.

Patients who have lost most or all of their teeth may benefit from Teeth in a Day dental implants, an advanced treatment often performed using the All-on-4 implant technique.
