What to expect at Auburn Centre Dental

Everything starts with a digital scan, not a tray of impression material. The iTero scanner maps your mouth in a few minutes with a small camera wand — a relief if impression moulds make you gag.

You’ll see the same team at each visit, we’ll tell you plainly how the adjustment period tends to go, and every cost is in writing before you commit to anything.

Questions people actually ask

Most people need a few weeks. Start with soft foods cut small, practise speaking out loud at home, and expect some sore spots early on — those mean the denture needs adjusting, not that you’re doing anything wrong. We adjust the fit until it settles.

Upper dentures usually hold by suction; lower dentures have less to grip and can shift, especially at first. If slipping is a real problem for you, implant-secured dentures snap onto two or more implants and stay put — our Dental Implants page explains that side of it.

A well-cared-for set commonly lasts five to ten years, but your mouth keeps changing shape the whole time. Regular relines keep the fit comfortable, and we check it at your routine visits.

We generally suggest taking them out at night to give your gums a rest, and cleaning them daily with a denture brush — not regular toothpaste, which can scratch the surface.

Full dentures replace every tooth in an upper or lower jaw and rest on the gums. Partial dentures fill the gaps when you still have healthy teeth, clipping onto the ones that remain. Implant-secured dentures snap onto implants placed in the jaw, so they don’t slip. Will people be able to tell I wear dentures? Each set is made to your measurements and to look like natural teeth. What usually gives dentures away is looseness, not appearance — which is why adjustments and relines matter.
