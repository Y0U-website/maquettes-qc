Orthodontics in Dorval, QC

If you are considering orthodontic treatment in Dorval, make an appointment at Dorval Dental Clinic for a consultation and to find the best treatment for you or your children. Schedule your Orthodontics Treatment consultation with us today!

  • Clear (ceramic) braces: made with a clear synthetic material and “tooth-colored” ceramic. They work in the same way as traditional braces, but are less visible and can be more comfortable. However, they are more susceptible to discoloration and staining.
  • Invisalign®: a system of clear, custom-made aligners that are worn on the teeth for the majority of the day to change the position of the teeth. They are practically invisible and the most comfortable orthodontic treatment option.
