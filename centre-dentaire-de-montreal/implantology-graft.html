Plasma-Rich fibrin (PRF) Stem Cell Therapy

PRF therapy uses a patient’s blood, which is processed to concentrate growth factors and stem cells. Consequently, it accelerates healing and enhances tissue regeneration in implant and grafting procedures.

  • PRF therapy for faster healing and tissue regeneration after implant placement or grafting
  • Use of natural growth factors to promote better bone and gum integration
  • Application of PRF in bone grafting, sinus lifts, and gum grafting procedures

Dental Implants (Single or Full Mouth)

Dental implants provide a permanent and stable solution to single-tooth replacements or full-mouth restorations.

  • Single-tooth implants for replacing individual missing teeth
  • Implant-supported bridges for replacing multiple missing teeth
  • Full-mouth implants for complete tooth replacement
  • All-on-4 or All-on-6 systems
  • Implant placement surgery using advanced imaging and planning
  • Custom crowns, bridges, and dentures for a natural, long-lasting result

Bone Grafting

Bone grafting is a surgical procedure that adds bone material to areas with insufficient bone mass, creating a stable foundation for dental implants.

  • Bone grafting procedures for patients with bone loss or insufficient bone structure
  • Advanced guided bone regeneration grafting techniques for bone augmentation and sinus lift surgeries
  • Post-operative care and monitoring for successful integration

Gum Grafting

Gum grafting is a procedure used to treat gum recession. It involves transplanting healthy connective tissues and PRF membranes to areas where gums have receded, improving gum health, teeth sensitivity, and aesthetics.

  • Soft tissue grafting to cover exposed tooth roots
  • Cosmetic gum grafting to enhance the appearance of the smile line
  • Functional grafting to improve gum stability and protect teeth from further recession
  • Post-surgical care to ensure proper healing
