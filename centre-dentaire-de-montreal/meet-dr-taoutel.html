Meet Dr. Taoutel

Dr. Chadi Taoutel is a distinguished dentist and a 1999 graduate of McGill University, where his journey in dentistry began. Following his graduation, he completed a multidisciplinary dental residency at the Centre Hospitalier de l’Université de Montréal (CHUM) in 1999 and at the Université de Montréal in 2000.

As an active member of the Ordre des Dentistes du Québec and the Association des Chirurgiens du Québec , Dr. Taoutel is deeply committed to his profession and continuous education that enhances his skills and knowledge. He serves as the Chief Editor of the Spectrum Quebec dental journal, contributing to improving dental practices and sharing valuable insights with his peers.

Dr. Taoutel’s dedication to continuous learning has taken him around the globe, allowing him to refine his expertise in various dental fields. At the Dawson Academy in Florida, he honed his occlusion and functional dental rehabilitation skills under exceptional mentorship. He also received specialized endodontic training from renowned leaders in the field , Dr. Buchanan and Dr. Machtou .

With over 25 years of experience in dentistry, Dr. Taoutel has had the privilege of helping countless patients regain their smiles. His expertise, combined with a deep compassion for his patients, defines his legacy. He strives to provide comfort, stability, and natural aesthetics for optimal oral health, ensuring that every individual leaves his practice with renewed confidence and a beautiful smile.
