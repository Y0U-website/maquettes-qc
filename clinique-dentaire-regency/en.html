Regency Dental Clinic, Downtown Montreal

We are a general dental practice located in downtown Montreal and we offer a multitude of services . If you are looking to improve your smile, relieve dental pain, or even for a routine checkup and fillings, then you have come to the right place.

We pride ourselves on earning an excellent reputation in the quality of treatment you receive and especially in the caring manner in which you receive them. Our clinicians are not only highly skilled dental practitioners, they also teach part-time at the MUHC residency clinic at the Montreal General Hospital in Montreal.
