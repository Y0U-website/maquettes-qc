Types of Implant Solutions We Offer

At Elite Dental Studio, we provide a full range of implant services so you can get the right solution for your needs—all in one familiar, trusted environment.

  • Single Dental Implants – Replace one missing tooth with a crown supported by an implant.
  • Implant-Supported Dentures – A stable, more comfortable alternative to traditional dentures.

The Dental Implant Process

  • Consultation & Planning – We’ll evaluate your oral health, review X-rays, and discuss your goals.
  • Implant Placement – The titanium post is gently placed into your jawbone.
  • Healing & Integration – Over the next few months, the implant fuses with your bone.
  • Restoration – A custom crown, bridge, or denture is attached for a permanent, natural smile.

FAQs About Dental Implants

All-On-4 Dental Implants

If you’re missing most or all of your teeth, dentures aren’t your only option. At Elite Dental Studio, we offer All-On-4 dental implants in Kirkland, WA—a treatment that can restore a full smile using just four implants per arch.

What Are All-On-4 Dental Implants?

All-On-4 combines the stability of implants with the convenience of full-arch restoration. Instead of placing an implant for each missing tooth, just four strategically positioned implants support a full arch of fixed replacement teeth.

  • A full, natural-looking smile with fewer implants
  • No slipping or adhesives like traditional dentures
  • Improved chewing power and comfort
  • Helps preserve jawbone health

The All-On-4 Process

  • Consultation – We review your oral health, jawbone density, and treatment goals.
  • Implant Placement – Four implants are placed at precise angles for maximum stability.
  • Temporary Teeth – In many cases, a temporary arch is placed the same day.
  • Final Restoration – Once healed, your permanent arch is secured, giving you a durable, confident smile.

FAQs About All-On-4

Most patients missing multiple teeth are good candidates. Even with some bone loss, All-On-4 often works without the need for bone grafting.

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The restoration may need replacement every 10–15 years.

We use advanced anesthesia and techniques to make the procedure as comfortable as possible. Most patients report less discomfort than expected.

Implant-Supported Dentures

If you’re tired of dentures that slip, irritate your gums, or make it difficult to chew, implant-supported dentures may be the solution. At Elite Dental Studio, we provide secure, comfortable dentures that attach directly to dental implants—giving you confidence, stability, and a smile you’ll love.

Why Choose Implant-Supported Dentures?

Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums, implant-supported dentures are anchored in place by dental implants. This added stability makes them feel and function more like natural teeth.

  • No slipping or clicking
  • Improved comfort and speech
  • Better chewing ability
  • Prevention of jawbone loss
  • Long-term durability

Types of Implant-Supported Dentures

Our team will help you determine which option is best for your lifestyle and oral health.

  • Fixed (Permanent) Dentures – Securely attached to implants and only removable by a dentist.
  • Removable Overdentures – Snap onto implants for easy removal and cleaning at home.

FAQs About Implant-Supported Dentures

Most cases require 2–6 implants per arch, depending on your treatment plan.

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The denture itself may need adjustment or replacement after several years.
