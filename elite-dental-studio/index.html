If you’re missing most or all of your teeth, dentures aren’t your only option. At Elite Dental Studio, we offer All-On-4 dental implants in Kirkland, WA—a treatment that can restore a full smile using just four implants per arch.

All-On-4 combines the stability of implants with the convenience of full-arch restoration. Instead of placing an implant for each missing tooth, just four strategically positioned implants support a full arch of fixed replacement teeth.

Most patients missing multiple teeth are good candidates. Even with some bone loss, All-On-4 often works without the need for bone grafting.

With proper care, the implants can last a lifetime. The restoration may need replacement every 10–15 years.

Dental implants are titanium posts surgically placed into the jawbone to act as artificial tooth roots. Once healed, they support a crown, bridge, or denture that looks and feels like your natural teeth.
