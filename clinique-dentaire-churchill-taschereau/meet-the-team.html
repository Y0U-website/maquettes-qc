Dr. Thimy D. Le, DMD

Dr. Thimy D. Le has been caring for patients for over three decades. Dr. Le is known for her gentle, thorough care, and ability to treat patients with complex needs, including those requiring sedation or comfort-focused care. She has extensive experience and is highly skilled in cosmetic dentistry, wisdom tooth extractions, dental implants, and conscious sedation techniques.

She is fluent in English, French, and Vietnamese, and speaks basic Spanish, which helps her connect with a diverse community of patients. Dr. Le is deeply committed to providing compassionate, personalized care that puts patients at ease—no matter their background or dental history.

Education

She earned her Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from the Universite de Montreal in 1989 and completed a residency at St. Mary’s Hospital in 1989-1990 where she started her implantology training. She further advanced her training in Geriatric Dentistry at McGill University, completed Pediatric Conscious Sedation at UCLA in California in 2002.

Dr. Le was awarded MeCCS by the ADSA, FICOI by the International Congress of Implantology, FAGD by the Academy of General Dentistry, and Associate Fellowship by the AAID. She received Pediatric Dentistry training as a recipient of the CDA Foundation and Evidence-Based Dentistry training as a recipient of ADA Foundation. Dr. Le has served on the editorial board of major dental organizations, and worked as both an expert consultant and a conscious sedation evaluator for the dental board. She is well-versed in Laser Dentistry.

General Dentistry

Dr. Thi Hanh Nguyen, DMD

With decades of experience, Dr. Nguyen is known for her compassionate care, attention to detail, and ability to connect with patients of all ages. She is fluent in English, French, and Vietnamese, and is deeply valued by her patients for her warmth, skill, and dedication to their oral health.

Education

Dr. Nguyen graduated from the University of Montreal Dental School in 1986 and became a member of the Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario in 1989. She completed additional training in orthodontics, followed by advanced implantology training at the Institut Dentaire Internationale (IDI) in 2013. She is well-versed in Laser Dentistry.
