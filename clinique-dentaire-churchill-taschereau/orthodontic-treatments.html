Orthodontic Care for the Whole Family

At Churchill Taschereau Dental Clinic, o ur orthodontic solutions are designed to align your teeth and improve your bite with comfort and discretion. From traditional braces to clear aligners, we offer options that fit your lifestyle while helping you achieve a healthier, straighter smile.

Explore Our Orthodontic Treatments Services

Traditional Braces Metal brackets and wires that gradually move teeth into better alignment. Suitable for both children and adults.

Clear Braces Tooth-colored or ceramic braces that function like metal braces but are less noticeable, offering a more aesthetic option.

Invisalign and Other Clear Aligner Options A clear aligner system that straightens teeth without metal braces. The aligners are removable, nearly invisible, and custom-made for comfort.

Removable appliances Devices like retainers or expanders used to maintain alignment or guide jaw development.
