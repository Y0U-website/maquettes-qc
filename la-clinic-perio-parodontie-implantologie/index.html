Le cabinet

A periodontist is a dental specialist who focuses on the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of gum disease and other conditions affecting the supporting structures of your teeth. Beyond treating gum problems, periodontists are experts in procedures such as dental implants and regenerative therapies, helping patients maintain healthy, strong, and confident smiles.
