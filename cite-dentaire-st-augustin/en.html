Treatments

Orthodontics

Discover orthodontics: a fascinating and multidisciplinary field that goes beyond simple aesthetic improvement

Denturology

Dental prostheses are an alternative to overcome the deficiencies associated with the loss of natural teeth.

Implantology

Dental implants and their restorations are a modern and effective advance in dentistry for the replacement of missing teeth.

Surgery

Wisdom tooth extractions prevent pain, infection, and dental misalignment; bone grafts prepare jaws for implants; frenectomies improve tongue mobility and oral aesthetics; and oral biopsies help diagnose and differentiate oral conditions for early treatment.

General dentistry

Dental restoration, commonly known as filling, is a frequently performed dental procedure aimed at repairing teeth damaged by cavities or fractures.

Periodontal care

Periodontal diseases are infections of the structures surrounding the teeth, including the gums, the periodontal ligament, and the alveolar bone.

Crowns and bridges

Dental crowns and bridges are dental procedures that are commonly used to restore the structural and functional integrity of damaged or missing teeth.

Preventive care

A discreet and modern approach to smoothing teeth using tailor-made aligners that are virtually invisible, offering comfort and flexibility.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal treatment involves removing damaged, infected, or dead dental pulp inside the tooth.

Cosmetic dentistry

Focuses on improving the appearance of your mouth, teeth, gums, and overall smile through various cosmetic dental procedures.
