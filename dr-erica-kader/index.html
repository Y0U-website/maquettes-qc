While many orthodontic treatments can start during the teen years, some children require earlier intervention in order to prevent problems from worsening, take advantage of growth, and increase the success of treatment later on.

Many orthodontic treatments are provided during the teen years, once all permanent teeth are in. This usually involves Braces or Invisalign to align the teeth and correct the bite relationship.

It is never too late to seek orthodontic treatment. Dr. Kader treats many adults with Braces or Invisalign, either for full treatment, or for limited treatment in conjunction with other dental treatments such as implants, crowns or veneers.

Invisalign is an orthodontic treatment modality that is a more esthetic alternative to regular braces, involving a series of custom-fabricated, clear, removable aligner trays. The aligners gradually move the teeth and are changed at prescribed intervals.

Regardless of which type of orthodontic treatment is performed, teeth have a tendency to move back toward their original position following treatment, and retainers are therefore provided to hold the orthodontic correction.
