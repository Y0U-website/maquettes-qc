Canadian Dental Care Program (CDCP)

We also offer the Canadian Dental Care Program (CDCP), which helps eligible patients reduce the cost of essential dental care. With this program, you can access affordable treatments like fillings, check-ups, and more, ensuring that your dental health is always taken care of without breaking the bank.
