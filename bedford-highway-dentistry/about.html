Meet Our Dentist

Committed to Excellence in Dentistry Your Smile, Our Priority

Dr. Brandon Lee ​

With over 10 years of experience running a successful dental practice in South Korea, Dr. Lee moved to Canada and obtained his dental license in 2022. After practicing in Vancouver for two years, he relocated to Halifax, where he discovered his passion for creating a practice centered on personalized, compassionate care.

Bedford Highway Dentistry was born from this vision, allowing Dr. Lee to build stronger relationships within the community and provide high-quality, tailored care to meet each patient’s unique needs.

Dr. Lee is a strong advocate for integrating technology into dentistry. He uses intraoral photography as a diagnostic tool to help patients better understand their oral health and treatment options. Committed to lifelong learning, he has pursued advanced training in areas such as dental implants, Invisalign, wisdom tooth extraction, root canal treatments, and Botox administration.

Outside of the clinic, Dr. Lee values spending quality time with his family. He enjoys swimming, badminton, and table tennis with his children, believing that these activities not only strengthen family bonds but also promote a healthy lifestyle. Dr. Lee is a firm believer in maintaining a healthy work-life balance to stay energized and focused, ensuring he can provide the best possible care for his patients.
