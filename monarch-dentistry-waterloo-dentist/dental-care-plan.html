Dental Care Plan

We’re thrilled to share an important update about Monarch Dentistry. As part of our dedication to offering accessible dental care to our community, we’re now accepting the Canadian Dental Care Plan (CDCP). This plan extends vital dental care coverage to eligible Canadian residents, including those with an adjusted family net income below $90,000 and without dental insurance access.

By embracing the CDCP, we’re committed to making high-quality oral healthcare services more within reach for a broader spectrum of individuals. We’re eager to expand our services to serve more community members and enhance their overall well-being.

If you have inquiries or need more details about the CDCP or our involvement, feel free to reach out to us. Our team is ready to assist you in any way possible.

Thank you for your attention, and we’re excited to continue working together to deliver exceptional dental care to our patients.
