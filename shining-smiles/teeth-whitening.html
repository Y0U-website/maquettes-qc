  • How many shades whiter can I expect my teeth to get? The amount of whitening varies from client to client and cannot be predicted or guaranteed. In general use, teeth may lighten anywhere from 6-12 shades Additional whitening treatments may be needed in order to achieve the desired shade, especially with heavily stained teeth
  • Will the treatment hurt? There shouldn’t be any pain, sensitivity, discomfort during or after the treatment. The whitening gel used contains a desensitizing agent that hydrates your teeth, which eliminates any sensitivity.
  • How do the sessions work? Will I have to come back multiple times? All sessions are completed in one appointment. After each session under the LED light, you will rinse with water and then repeat the process.
  • How long will my results last? A touch-up is recommended every 3-6 months. However, results will last depending on your daily habits (drinking coffee, red wine, smoking, etc).
  • Is cosmetic teeth whitening safe? Our whitening gel is 100 % safe for your enamel and any dental work you have done (fillings, bridges, crowns, etc). The gel we use is approved by the FDA and Health Canada.
  • Do not consume staining agents such as coffee, tea, red wine, colas, tobacco, etc for 24 hours after your appointment
  • A touch up is recommended every 3-6 months
  • We recommend clients see their dental hygienist every 6 months and have a year dental check up every year to ensure good oral health
