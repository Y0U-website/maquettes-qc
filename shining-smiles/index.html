Policies Before booking, please take a moment to review our policies so you know what to expect before your appointment. Our policies help ensure a smooth, professional, and enjoyable experience for every client, including important information about bookings, cancellations, late arrivals, and appointment preparation. By booking, you agree to the policies listed below.

Any cancellation or rescheduling must have a minimum of 24 hours notice prior to the appointment. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice will result in a loss of deposit. A new deposit will need to be sent in order to rebook.

If you are more than 15 minutes late, a $10 fee will be applied to the service. If you are more than 30 minutes late, your appointment will be cancelled.

Please do not bring guests with you to your appointment without prior approval.
