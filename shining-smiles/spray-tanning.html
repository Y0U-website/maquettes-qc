  • Focus on rough areas like elbows, knees, ankles, feet, knuckles, and underarms.
  • Proper exfoliation helps your tan apply evenly and last longer.
  • Avoid products On the day of your appointment, avoid: Lotion
  • Makeup
  • Sunscreen
  • Perfume
  • Deodorant
  • Oil-based products
  • Moisturizing tips Moisturize your skin in the days leading up to your appointment.
  • Do not moisturize on the day of your session.
  • What to wear You may wear undergarments, a bathing suit, or go without during your application.
  • Wear loose, dark clothing to your appointment to avoid transfer and streaking afterward.

After your session

  • During development time Avoid sweating or contact with water while your tan develops.
  • Avoid applying lotions, makeup, or creams.
  • If it’s raining or snowing, wear long, loose clothing.
  • Sleeping with the solution on? Wear long sleeves and pants to protect your tan.
  • Showering Wait 1–5 hours or 8–12 hours before rinsing, depending on your solution.
  • Rinse thoroughly until the water runs clear.
  • Avoid soap during your first rinse.
  • Maintaining your tan Moisturize daily to extend the life of your tan.
  • Use gentle, non-exfoliating skincare products.
  • Avoid products containing mineral oil, petroleum, or alcohol.
  • When your tan starts to fade, exfoliate again to remove any remaining residue and prepare your skin for your next glow.
  • Avoid for longer lasting results Hot baths and long showers
  • Jacuzzis and saunas
  • Excessive sweating
  • Exfoliating products
  • Waxing treated areas
